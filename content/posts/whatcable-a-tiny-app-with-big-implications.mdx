## Introduction

The recent release of WhatCable, a tiny menu bar app for inspecting USB-C cables, has taken the Hacker News community by storm. Developed by Darryl Morley, WhatCable provides a simple yet effective way to uncover the capabilities and limitations of USB-C cables. In this post, we'll delve into the story behind WhatCable, its significance for developers, and the broader implications for the tech industry.

## The Story Behind WhatCable

WhatCable is an open-source app available on GitHub, allowing users to inspect their USB-C cables and determine their specifications, such as speed, power delivery, and alternate modes. The app is incredibly easy to use, requiring only a simple plug-and-play action to retrieve the desired information. This level of transparency is a game-changer for developers, who often struggle with debugging and optimizing their hardware projects due to unclear or misleading cable specifications.
