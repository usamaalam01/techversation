## What's Happening
El Niño is a complex weather phenomenon characterized by the warming of ocean water in the eastern Pacific. This warming of ocean water has a ripple effect on global weather patterns, leading to extreme weather events such as droughts, floods, and heatwaves. The current El Niño event is the strongest on record, with temperatures in the Pacific exceeding 3°C above average.
