## Introduction to the Debate

A recent post on [Hacker News](https://blog.jim-nielsen.com/2026/blogging-stating-the-obvious/) has ignited a discussion about the nature of blogging, particularly in the context of technical writing. The author suggests that blogging can, and sometimes should, be about stating the obvious. This idea has sparked a range of reactions from the community, with some embracing the concept and others questioning its value.

## Why It Matters to Developers

For developers, the idea that blogging can be about stating the obvious may seem counterintuitive. After all, technical writing is often associated with complex, in-depth explanations of cutting-edge concepts. However, the reality is that many developers are looking for straightforward, easy-to-understand information that can help them solve everyday problems. By sharing obvious knowledge, bloggers can provide a valuable service to their readers, even if the information seems simple.

### The Value of Simplicity

Simplicity is a key aspect of effective technical writing. When bloggers focus on stating the obvious, they can create content that is accessible to a wider audience. This approach can be particularly helpful for junior developers or those who are new to a particular field. By providing clear, concise explanations of fundamental concepts, bloggers can help to fill the knowledge gap and create a more supportive community.

## Broader Implications

The idea that blogging can be about stating the obvious has broader implications for the way we approach technical writing. It suggests that we should prioritize clarity and accessibility over complexity and novelty. By doing so, we can create a more inclusive community that values simplicity and ease of understanding.

### Community Sentiment

The reaction to the post on Hacker News reflects the diversity of opinions within the community. Some commenters have expressed support for the idea, noting that simplicity is often underrated in technical writing. Others have questioned the value of stating the obvious, arguing that it can be a waste of time and resources. As one commenter noted, "If it's obvious, why bother writing about it?"

However, this perspective overlooks the fact that what may be obvious to one person may not be obvious to another. By sharing knowledge, even if it seems straightforward, bloggers can help to create a more supportive and inclusive community.

## Conclusion

The debate about whether blogging can be about stating the obvious highlights the complexities of technical writing. While some may view simplicity as a weakness, it can also be a strength, particularly when it comes to creating accessible and easy-to-understand content. As developers and writers, we should prioritize clarity and accessibility, even if it means stating the obvious. By doing so, we can create a more supportive and inclusive community that values simplicity and ease of understanding. 

In the world of technical writing, it's not always about breaking new ground or exploring cutting-edge concepts. Sometimes, it's about providing clear, concise explanations of fundamental ideas. And that's okay. As we move forward, it's essential to recognize the value of simplicity and to prioritize accessibility in our writing. Only then can we create a community that truly supports and empowers all its members.
