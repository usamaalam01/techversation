## Introduction to the Mission

NASA has selected Relativity Space, a rocket company backed by former Google executive Eric Schmidt, to launch its Aeolus payload to Mars in 2028, as [reported by The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/science/952988/nasa-relativity-space-eric-schmidt-mars). This decision is a significant milestone in the collaboration between public and private entities in space exploration. Under this partnership, Relativity Space will be responsible for providing the spacecraft, rocket, and cruise operations necessary to fly the Aeolus payload to Mars.

## Why It Matters to Developers

The selection of Relativity Space for this mission is noteworthy for several reasons. Firstly, it underscores the growing importance of private companies in space technology and exploration. Relativity Space's involvement in this project demonstrates the capabilities of private enterprises in developing and launching complex space missions. This shift towards public-private partnerships can lead to more efficient and cost-effective space exploration, as private companies can bring innovative solutions and agile development methodologies to the table.

For developers, this partnership is particularly interesting because it highlights the role of digital technology in space exploration. The development of spacecraft, rockets, and associated systems relies heavily on advanced software and hardware technologies. As such, developers with expertise in areas like embedded systems, robotics, and data analytics will be crucial in supporting future space missions.
