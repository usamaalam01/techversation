## Introduction to the New Policy
The Trump administration has introduced a new policy aimed at restricting legal immigration pathways, specifically targeting green cards. According to a statement from the Department of Homeland Security, this policy change is intended to "allow our immigration system to function as the law intended instead of incentivizing loopholes." However, the implications of this policy are far-reaching and may have significant consequences for the tech industry, which relies heavily on international talent.

## Impact on the Tech Industry
The tech industry has long been a proponent of increased immigration, recognizing the value that international talent brings to the development of new technologies and innovation. Many top tech companies, including Google, Facebook, and Microsoft, have spoken out in support of more open immigration policies, citing the need for skilled workers to fill key roles. The new policy may lead to increased scrutiny of adjustment of status applications, making it more difficult for international workers to obtain green cards and ultimately, citizenship.
