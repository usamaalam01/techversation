## What is ByHand.ai?

ByHand.ai is a no-code AI development platform that allows users to design, train, and deploy AI models using a visual interface. The platform provides a range of tools and features that enable users to create complex AI models without requiring extensive programming knowledge. This approach has the potential to democratize AI development, making it more accessible to a broader range of users.
