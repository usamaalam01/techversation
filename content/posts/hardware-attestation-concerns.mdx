## Introduction to Hardware Attestation

The recent discussion on [Hacker News](https://grapheneos.social/@GrapheneOS/116550899908879585) surrounding hardware attestation as a potential monopoly enabler has sparked a heated debate among developers and tech enthusiasts. At its core, hardware attestation is a security feature designed to verify the integrity and authenticity of a device's hardware and software components. However, as highlighted by GrapheneOS, this technology can also be used to restrict competition and limit consumer choice.

## Why It Matters to Developers

For developers, the implications of hardware attestation as a monopoly enabler are significant. If a single company or group of companies can control the attestation process, they may be able to dictate which devices and software are allowed to operate on their platforms. This could lead to a lack of innovation and diversity in the market, as smaller companies and independent developers may be shut out. Furthermore, it could also limit the ability of developers to create custom or open-source solutions, as these may not meet the attestation requirements set by the dominant players.
