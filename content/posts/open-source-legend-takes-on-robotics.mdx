## Introduction to Jean-Baptiste Kempf's Latest Venture

Jean-Baptiste Kempf, the French serial entrepreneur behind the widely-used VLC media player, has set his sights on a new challenge: building an infrastructure layer to control remote devices in real time. As reported by [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/19/he-made-your-free-video-player-run-smoothly-now-hes-doing-that-for-robots/), Kempf's latest project, Kyber, aims to provide a robust and efficient way to manage remote devices, with a focus on robotics and automation.

## Why It Matters to Developers

For developers, Kyber represents an exciting opportunity to explore new possibilities in robotics and automation. By providing a standardized infrastructure layer for controlling remote devices, Kyber can simplify the development process and enable the creation of more complex and sophisticated systems. This, in turn, can lead to innovations in various fields, such as manufacturing, healthcare, and transportation.

### The Potential of Kyber

Kyber's potential extends beyond robotics and automation. Its infrastructure layer can be applied to a wide range of industries, including:

* **Smart Homes**: Kyber can be used to control and integrate various smart devices, creating a seamless and efficient home automation experience.
* **Industrial Automation**: By providing real-time control and monitoring capabilities, Kyber can help optimize industrial processes, reducing downtime and increasing productivity.
* **Healthcare**: Kyber can be used to control medical devices, such as robotic surgical systems, and enable remote monitoring and diagnosis.
