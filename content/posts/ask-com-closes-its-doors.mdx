## Introduction to Ask.com

Ask.com, a search engine that was once a major player in the online search market, has closed its doors. The website, which was founded in 1996 as Ask Jeeves, was known for its question-answering format and was a popular alternative to Google and other search engines. Over the years, Ask.com has undergone several changes, including a shift in focus from search to question-answering and a series of acquisitions and mergers.

## What Happened

According to reports on [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/), Ask.com's website is no longer operational, and users are being redirected to other search engines. The exact reasons for the shutdown are not clear, but it is likely due to a combination of factors, including increased competition from other search engines and a decline in usage.

## Why It Matters to Developers

The shutdown of Ask.com matters to developers for several reasons. Firstly, Ask.com provided a range of APIs and services that developers could use to integrate search functionality into their applications. These APIs included the Ask.com Search API, which allowed developers to access Ask.com's search index and retrieve search results programmatically. With the shutdown of Ask.com, these APIs are no longer available, and developers who relied on them will need to find alternative solutions.


## Alternative Solutions
