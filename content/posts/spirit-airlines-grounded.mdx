## Introduction

In a shocking turn of events, Spirit Airlines, a popular ultra-low-cost air carrier, has shut down its operations after 34 years in business. The sudden closure, which occurred at 3AM ET on Saturday morning, has left thousands of passengers stranded and employees without jobs. According to a report by [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/business/922788/spirit-airlines-shutdown), the airline's website now redirects to spiritrestructuring.com, instructing fliers not to go to airports.

## What Happened

The shutdown is attributed to the significant increase in jet fuel prices, which have more than doubled since the start of the conflict between the US and Iran. As reported by [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/business/922788/spirit-airlines-shutdown), this surge in fuel costs has made it impossible for Spirit Airlines to continue operating. The airline, known for its budget-friendly fares, was particularly vulnerable to fluctuations in fuel prices.
