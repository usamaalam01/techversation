## Introduction
In a surprising revelation, the US Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) has admitted to building its incident response playbook during an actual security incident. This news has significant implications for the cybersecurity community, highlighting the importance of preparedness and the need for well-planned incident response strategies.

## What Happened
According to a report by [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/10/us-cyber-agency-cisa-had-to-build-its-incident-playbook-during-the-incident-agency-reveals/), a security researcher from GitGuardian discovered a publicly accessible GitHub repository containing exposed passwords uploaded by an employee of a CISA contractor. This incident led to CISA having to create its incident response playbook on the fly, which raises concerns about the agency's preparedness to handle such incidents.

This incident matters to developers and the broader cybersecurity community for several reasons. Firstly, it highlights the importance of having a well-planned incident response strategy in place.
