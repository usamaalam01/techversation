## Introduction to Mira Murati's Vision

Mira Murati, the founder of Thinking Machines Lab and former CTO of OpenAI, has been making waves in the AI community with her unique approach to AI development. In a recent interview with [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/mira-murati-humans-in-the-loop-ai-models-thinking-machines/), Murati emphasized the importance of keeping humans in the loop when it comes to AI. This approach is a significant departure from the conventional wisdom that AI is meant to automate tasks and replace human workers.

## Why Human Collaboration Matters

Murati's vision is centered around the idea that AI should be designed to collaborate with humans, rather than replace them. This approach is rooted in the understanding that human intuition, creativity, and empathy are essential components of many tasks, and that AI systems can augment and support human capabilities rather than replacing them. By keeping humans in the loop, AI systems can be designed to be more transparent, explainable, and accountable.
