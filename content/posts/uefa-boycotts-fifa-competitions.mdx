## Introduction to the UEFA-FIFA Dispute
The Union of European Football Associations (UEFA) and its 55 national associations have announced that they will not participate in FIFA competitions. This decision comes after a long-standing dispute between the two organizations. The news was reported on the [UEFA website](https://www.uefa.com/news-media/news/02a7-213a92896eb0-54dfbf454e3b-1000--statement-on-behalf-of-uefa-and-its-55-national-associations/), stating that the decision was made due to disagreements over various issues.
