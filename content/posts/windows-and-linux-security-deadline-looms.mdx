## Introduction to the Deadline
A critical deadline is approaching for Windows and Linux security, with cryptographic keys that secure the boot sequence of computers set to expire on June 24, as reported by [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/a-critical-deadline-is-approaching-for-windows-and-linux-security/). This change has significant implications for developers and users, as it affects the fundamental security of their systems.

## What's Happening
The cryptographic keys in question are used to verify the integrity of the boot sequence, ensuring that the operating system and other software are legitimate and have not been tampered with. When these keys expire, systems may fail to boot or may become vulnerable to security threats. This is a critical issue, as it has the potential to affect millions of computers worldwide.
