## Introduction to the Study
A recent study published in the journal Sleep has made a groundbreaking discovery: sleep regularity is a stronger predictor of mortality risk than sleep duration. This finding has significant implications for our understanding of sleep and its impact on overall health. As developers, we can use this knowledge to create more effective health and wellness applications.

## Understanding the Study
The study, which can be found on the [Oxford Academic website](https://academic.oup.com/sleep/article/47/1/zsad253/7280269), analyzed data from over 2,000 adults and found that irregular sleep patterns were associated with a higher risk of mortality, regardless of the total amount of sleep. This suggests that consistency is key when it comes to sleep, and that developers can use this information to create applications that promote healthy sleep habits.

## Why it Matters to Developers
As developers, we are often tasked with creating applications that promote healthy habits and behaviors. This study provides valuable insights into the importance of sleep regularity, and how we can use this information to create more effective health and wellness applications. For example, we can create apps that track sleep patterns and provide personalized recommendations for improving sleep regularity.

```python
import pandas as pd

# Sample data
data = {
    'sleep_duration': [7, 8, 9, 10],
    'sleep_regularity': [0.5, 0.6, 0.7, 0.8]
}

# Create a DataFrame
df = pd.DataFrame(data)

# Calculate the mortality risk based on sleep regularity
df['mortality_risk'] = df['sleep_regularity'].apply(lambda x: 1 - x)

print(df)
```
