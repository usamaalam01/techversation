## Introduction
On May 19, 2026, Railway, a popular platform for building and deploying applications, was blocked due to a Google Cloud outage. The incident affected many developers who rely on Railway for their daily work. In this post, we will summarize what happened, explain why it matters to developers, and provide our take on the broader implications.

## What Happened
According to the incident report published on Railway's blog, the outage was caused by a Google Cloud account issue that prevented Railway from accessing its resources. The report states that the issue was resolved after Railway's team worked with Google Cloud to resolve the account issue. You can read the full incident report on [Railway's blog](https://blog.railway.com/p/incident-report-may-19-2026-gcp-account-outage).

## Why it Matters to Developers
The incident highlights the risks of relying on cloud services for application development and deployment. As developers, we often take for granted the availability of cloud services, assuming that they will always be up and running. However, outages like this one remind us that even the most reliable services can fail. This is why it's essential to have a backup plan in place, such as using multiple cloud providers or having a local development environment.

## Broader Implications
The incident has broader implications for the cloud computing industry. It highlights the need for cloud providers to have robust backup systems in place to prevent outages. It also highlights the need for developers to diversify their cloud providers and have a backup plan in place.

### Conclusion
In conclusion, the Google Cloud outage that blocked Railway is a reminder of the risks of relying on cloud services. It highlights the importance of having a backup plan in place and the need for cloud providers to have robust backup systems. As developers, we must be aware of these risks and take steps to mitigate them.
