## Why is Meta restructuring its engineering organization?

According to the report, Meta is restructuring its engineering organization to improve efficiency and reduce bureaucracy. The company aims to streamline its decision-making process, eliminate redundant roles, and focus on key areas such as artificial intelligence, virtual reality, and e-commerce. While the exact details of the restructuring are not publicly available, it's clear that Meta is attempting to adapt to the changing tech landscape and stay competitive.

```python
# Example of a simplified organizational structure
class EngineeringOrganization:
    def __init__(self):
        self.teams = []

    def add_team(self, team):
        self.teams.append(team)

    def remove_team(self, team):
        self.teams.remove(team)

# Create an instance of the organization
org = EngineeringOrganization()

# Add teams
org.add_team("AI Team")
org.add_team("VR Team")
org.add_team("E-commerce Team")

# Remove redundant teams
org.remove_team("Redundant Team")
```
