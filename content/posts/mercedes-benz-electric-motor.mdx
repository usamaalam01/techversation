## What is an Axial Flux Motor?

An axial flux motor is a type of electric motor that uses a unique design to improve efficiency and performance. Unlike traditional radial flux motors, which have the magnetic field lines perpendicular to the axis of rotation, axial flux motors have the magnetic field lines parallel to the axis of rotation. This design allows for a more compact and efficient motor, making it an attractive option for electric vehicles.
