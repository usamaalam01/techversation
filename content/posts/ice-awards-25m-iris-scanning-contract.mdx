## Introduction to the Contract
The US Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) has recently awarded a $25 million contract to Bi2 Technologies for the development and implementation of iris-scanning technology. This move is part of ICE's efforts to enhance its biometric capabilities and improve the efficiency of its operations. As reported on [Hacker News](https://www.projectsaltbox.com/p/ice-awards-25-million-iris-scanning), the contract has sparked interest and concern among developers, privacy advocates, and the general public.

## What Happened
Bi2 Technologies, a company specializing in biometric solutions, will provide ICE with iris-scanning technology to be used in various applications, including identity verification and surveillance. The contract is valued at $25 million and is expected to be completed over a period of several years. The exact details of the contract, including the scope of work and the specific technologies to be used, have not been publicly disclosed.
