## Introduction

Online advertising has become a crucial aspect of modern marketing, with companies spending billions of dollars on digital ads every year. However, traditional online advertising bidding systems often rely on offline-trained expert models, which can be limited by their lack of online adaptability and reliance on manual hyperparameter tuning. To address these limitations, researchers have proposed a new approach called Hierarchical On-Policy Bidding Agents (HOBA).

## What is HOBA?

HOBA is a hierarchical on-policy bidding agent designed for adaptive online advertising. It consists of a hierarchical structure of bidding agents, each responsible for a specific aspect of the bidding process. The agents learn to bid optimally using reinforcement learning, allowing them to adapt to changing market conditions and automate hyperparameter tuning.
