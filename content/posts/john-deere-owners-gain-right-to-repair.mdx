## Introduction to the Right-to-Repair Movement

The right-to-repair movement has been gaining momentum in recent years, with consumers and advocates pushing for the ability to repair and maintain their own products, rather than relying on manufacturers. This movement has been driven by concerns over consumer choice, sustainability, and the environment. The recent settlement between John Deere and the Federal Trade Commission (FTC) is a significant development in this movement, as it grants John Deere owners the right to repair their equipment.

## What Happened

According to a report by the [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/john-deere-right-to-repair-agriculture-equipment-cb7514ffedb95c130a976af661f2bc02), John Deere has reached a settlement with the FTC, which requires the company to provide owners and independent repair shops with access to diagnostic tools, repair manuals, and other resources needed to repair John Deere equipment. This settlement is seen as a major win for the right-to-repair movement, as it sets a precedent for other manufacturers to follow.
