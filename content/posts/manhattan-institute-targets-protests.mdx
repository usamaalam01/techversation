## Introduction to the Manhattan Institute's Campaign
The Manhattan Institute, a right-wing think tank, has been actively promoting policies that aim to increase penalties for minor crimes committed during protests, which they term as "civil terrorism." According to a recent article in [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/the-manhattan-institute-helped-kill-dei-now-its-coming-for-protests/), this campaign is the latest in a series of efforts by the think tank to shape public policy and discourse. 

## Background and Previous Efforts
The Manhattan Institute has previously been involved in campaigns that targeted diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI) initiatives, particularly in the context of technology and education. Their efforts have been successful in influencing public opinion and policy decisions, often by framing their arguments in terms of promoting "meritocracy" and opposing "identity politics."
