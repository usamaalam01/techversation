## Introduction to the Recall
The US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has recently [announced](https://www.wired.com/story/sexual-chocolate-recalls-fda-undisclosed-viagra/) the recall of several chocolate products due to the presence of undisclosed Viagra ingredients. These products, including Boner Bears and DTF, were found to contain the active ingredients in Viagra and Cialis, which are prescription medications used to treat erectile dysfunction.
