## Introduction to Rivian's Data Collection Policy

Rivian, an electric vehicle manufacturer, has recently published an article on their support page titled "Can I disable all data collection from my vehicle?" This article aims to address customer concerns about the type of data collected by their vehicles and how it is used. As a developer, it's essential to understand the implications of data collection in the automotive industry and how it affects user privacy.

## What Happened

According to the article, Rivian collects various types of data from their vehicles, including vehicle performance, location, and usage patterns. This data is used to improve vehicle performance, provide maintenance alerts, and offer personalized recommendations to drivers. However, some customers have expressed concerns about the amount of data being collected and the potential risks to their privacy. In response, Rivian has provided options for customers to opt-out of certain data collection features, such as location-based services and personalized advertising.
