## Introduction to the Crisis

A [recent article in Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/heat-wave-schizophrenia/) sheds light on the alarming relationship between heat waves and schizophrenia. The research reveals that individuals with schizophrenia are more susceptible to heat-related illnesses and deaths compared to the general population. This increased vulnerability is largely due to the side effects of antipsychotic medications, which can impair the body's ability to regulate temperature. Furthermore, social factors such as poverty, lack of access to cool environments, and social isolation exacerbate the risks.

## Why It Matters to Developers

As software developers and AI practitioners, it's essential to recognize the intersection of technology, climate change, and mental health. The development of digital solutions can play a critical role in addressing the unique challenges faced by individuals with schizophrenia during heat waves. For instance, AI-powered systems can help identify high-risk individuals, predict heat wave events, and provide personalized recommendations for staying cool and safe. Moreover, technology can facilitate access to mental health services, social support, and cooling resources, thereby mitigating the social determinants that contribute to heat-related vulnerabilities.

## The Role of Technology in Mitigation

Technology can be a powerful tool in reducing the risks associated with heat waves and schizophrenia. For example, mobile apps can be developed to provide individuals with schizophrenia with personalized heat wave alerts, cooling tips, and reminders to stay hydrated. Additionally, AI-powered chatbots can offer social support and connect users with mental health resources during extreme weather events.
