## Introduction to Kompas VC's Strategy

In a recent article published on [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/28/how-one-venture-firm-is-navigating-an-increasingly-fragmented-world/), it was revealed that Kompas VC is taking a unique approach to venture investing amidst the current geopolitical turmoil. The firm has decided to focus on startups that are working on solutions for the physical world, rather than solely on digital technologies. This strategy is a deliberate attempt to carve out a niche in a world where digital fragmentation is increasingly becoming a challenge.

## Why This Matters to Developers

As developers, it's essential to understand the implications of this strategy and how it might affect the broader tech industry. By focusing on the physical world, Kompas VC is essentially betting on the idea that there will be a renewed interest in technologies that can solve real-world problems, such as infrastructure, transportation, and energy. This shift in focus could lead to increased investment in areas like IoT, robotics, and sustainable technologies.
