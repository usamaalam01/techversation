## Introduction to Dyson's Find+Follow Purifier Cool

Dyson has announced its latest innovation: the Find+Follow Purifier Cool, an air purifying bladeless fan equipped with an AI-powered camera for tracking people's location in a room. This device is the company's first to incorporate such technology, which is already used in its robovacs. According to [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/930348/dyson-find-follow-air-purifier-cool-ai-tracking-camera-privacy), the camera allows the purifier to follow individuals around the room, ensuring they receive fresh air.
