## Introduction to the Acquisition

On Monday, Fox announced its plan to acquire Roku, a leading streaming middleman, for $22 billion. This deal is expected to give Fox control over the TVs in more than 100 million homes worldwide. As a developer, you might be wondering what this means for the future of streaming and TV technology.

## Why it Matters to Developers

The acquisition of Roku by Fox is significant for developers because it may lead to changes in the way streaming services are integrated and consumed. Roku's platform currently supports a wide range of streaming services, including Netflix, Disney Plus, and Hulu. If Fox decides to prioritize its own content and services, developers may need to adapt to new APIs and integration requirements. This could lead to additional complexity and costs for developers who want to distribute their content through Roku's platform.

### Impact on Streaming Services

The deal may also impact the way streaming services are discovered and consumed by users. Fox may choose to promote its own content and services over others, which could lead to a decrease in visibility for smaller streaming services. This could be a challenge for developers who are trying to reach a wider audience with their content.

## Broader Implications

The acquisition of Roku by Fox is part of a larger trend of consolidation in the TV and streaming industry. As more companies acquire and merge with each other, it may lead to a decrease in competition and innovation. This could ultimately harm consumers who may have fewer choices and higher prices for streaming services.
