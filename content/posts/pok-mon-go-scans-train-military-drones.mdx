## Introduction to Pokémon Go Scans and Military Drones

A recent article on [Dronexl](https://dronexl.co/2026/06/09/pokemon-go-scans-niantic-vantor-military-drone-navigation/) has revealed that scans from the popular mobile game Pokémon Go have been used to train navigation technology for military drones. This news may come as a surprise to many, but it highlights the innovative ways in which data from unexpected sources can be used to advance technologies.

## What Happened

The scans in question were collected by Niantic, the company behind Pokémon Go, as part of the game's augmented reality features. These scans were then used to train machine learning models that enable drones to navigate complex environments. The technology, developed by Vantor, uses a combination of lidar and camera data to create detailed maps of surroundings, allowing drones to avoid obstacles and navigate with greater precision.
