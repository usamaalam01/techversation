## Introduction

The European Union has fined Temu, an e-commerce platform, €200M for allowing the sale of illegal products on its website. This move is a significant development in the ongoing debate about the role of e-commerce companies in regulating online marketplaces. As reported by the [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c1k2ydn1rz8o), the fine is a result of Temu's failure to comply with EU regulations regarding the sale of illegal products.

Developers must ensure that their platforms are designed to prevent the sale of illegal products and comply with relevant regulations. ### Regulatory Compliance

Regulatory compliance is a critical aspect of e-commerce development. Developers must stay up-to-date with the latest regulations and ensure that their platforms comply with relevant laws. This includes implementing measures to prevent the sale of illegal products, such as:

* Content moderation tools to detect and remove illegal products
* Verification processes to ensure seller authenticity
* Reporting mechanisms to alert authorities to suspicious activity

```python
# Example of a content moderation tool
import re

def moderate_content(product_description):
    # Define a list of banned keywords
    banned_keywords = ["illegal", "prohibited"]
    
    # Check if any banned keywords are present in the product description
    for keyword in banned_keywords:
        if re.search(keyword, product_description):
            return False
    
    return True
```
