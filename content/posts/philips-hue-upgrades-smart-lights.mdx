## Introduction to Philips Hue's Connectivity Upgrade

Philips Hue, a leading smart lighting brand, has recently announced a significant connectivity upgrade for its smart bulbs. According to a report by [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/955767/philips-hue-thread-matter-zigbee-update-silicon-labs), the upgrade will enable Philips Hue's Matter-over-Thread-enabled smart bulbs to run both Thread and Zigbee at the same time. This change means that compatible Hue bulbs and fixtures can now be connected directly to a Matter ecosystem, such as Apple Home, Amazon Alexa, or Google Home, while maintaining their existing Zigbee connections.

## Why it Matters to Developers

For developers, this upgrade is significant because it simplifies the process of integrating Philips Hue smart bulbs with various smart home ecosystems. With the ability to run both Thread and Zigbee, developers can create more seamless and interoperable smart home experiences.
