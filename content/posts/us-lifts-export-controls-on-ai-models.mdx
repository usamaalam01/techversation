## Background

The US government has been cautious in its approach to AI exports, citing concerns over national security and the potential for AI technology to be used for malicious purposes. As a result, many AI models have been subject to export controls, limiting their availability to certain countries and entities. However, with the rapid advancement of AI technology, there has been growing pressure to relax these controls and allow for greater collaboration and innovation.
