## Introduction

A recent report on Hacker News has shed light on a disturbing trend in the AI industry: the destruction of rare books for training data. According to the report, AI companies are shredding rare and valuable books to feed their machine learning models, sparking outrage among book lovers, conservationists, and developers. In this post, we'll delve into the story, explore why it matters to developers, and examine the broader implications of this practice.

## The Story Behind the Shredding

The report, which was shared on social media by HedgieMarkets, claims that AI companies are acquiring rare and valuable books, only to shred them and use the resulting text data to train their models. This practice is not only destroying cultural artifacts but also raising concerns about the ethics of AI development. The use of rare books as a source of training data is not a new phenomenon, but the scale and intent behind this practice are alarming.

## Why It Matters to Developers

As developers, we have a responsibility to consider the impact of our actions on the world around us. While AI has the potential to revolutionize numerous industries and improve lives, it's essential to ensure that our methods are ethical and sustainable.

The use of rare books as a source of training data is not only a loss for cultural preservation but also a missed opportunity for collaboration between the AI and conservation communities. Open-source and crowdsourcing initiatives can play a significant role in promoting sustainable AI development.
