## Introduction to Akashic
Akashic is a low-overhead LLM (Large Language Model) inference service designed to efficiently handle context accumulation across multi-turn interactions, tool invocations, and cross-session workflows. The traditional approach of replaying the full history for every request becomes impractical due to increased prefill cost, context limits, and degradation of task-relevant evidence. Akashic addresses these challenges by introducing MemAttention, a mechanism that enables efficient context handling.

## Understanding MemAttention
MemAttention is a key component of Akashic, allowing the model to focus on relevant context while ignoring irrelevant information. This is achieved through a combination of memory-augmented attention and sparse attention mechanisms. By leveraging MemAttention, Akashic can reduce the overhead associated with context handling, resulting in improved inference efficiency.
