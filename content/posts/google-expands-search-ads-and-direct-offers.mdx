## Introduction

Google has announced that it is testing new ad formats in Search and expanding its Direct Offers pilot. This move is expected to have a significant impact on the way businesses advertise on Google and how users interact with search results. In this post, we will analyze the implications of this development and what it means for developers and the broader tech community.

## What's Changing

The new ad formats being tested in Search aim to provide more visually appealing and interactive ads that can help businesses stand out. These ads will include features such as images, videos, and carousels, making them more engaging and immersive for users. Additionally, the expansion of the Direct Offers pilot will enable more merchants to offer deals and discounts directly to customers through Google Search.
