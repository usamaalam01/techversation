## Introduction to the Discount

LG has recently announced a promo code that offers a 20% discount on various products, including appliances and TVs. According to a report by [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/lg-promo-code/), customers can save up to $1,000 on appliances and 40% off bestselling TVs and monitors. This move may seem like a straightforward marketing strategy, but it has implications for the tech industry as a whole.

Discounts like the one offered by LG can affect the demand for certain products and technologies. For example, if LG's discount leads to a surge in sales of their TVs, it may indicate a shift in consumer preferences towards larger screens or specific features. This, in turn, can influence the types of projects we work on and the technologies we use.

```python
# Example of how discounts can affect sales
class Product:
    def __init__(self, price, discount):
        self.price = price
        self.discount = discount

    def calculate_price(self):
        return self.price * (1 - self.discount)

# Create a product with a 20% discount
product = Product(1000, 0.2)
print(product.calculate_price())  # Output: 800.0
```
