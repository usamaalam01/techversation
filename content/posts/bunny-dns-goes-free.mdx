In a move that is sending shockwaves through the tech community, Bunny DNS has announced that it is making its service free. This decision is a significant development, not just for the company, but for the entire internet ecosystem. As a key component of the internet's infrastructure, DNS (Domain Name System) plays a crucial role in determining the speed and reliability of online services. By making Bunny DNS free, the company is aiming to contribute to a faster and more efficient internet.

## What Happened

The announcement was made on the [Bunny DNS blog](https://bunny.net/blog/were-making-bunny-dns-free/), where the company outlined its vision for a faster internet. According to the post, Bunny DNS believes that a faster internet won't build itself, and that making its service free is a key step towards achieving this goal. The company is confident that by providing a high-quality, free DNS service, it can help to accelerate the growth of the internet and improve the overall user experience.
