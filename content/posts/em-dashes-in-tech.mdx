But why does it matter, and what does it reveal about the community's values and priorities?

## The Importance of Em Dashes

Em dashes are used to set off parenthetical phrases, add emphasis, and clarify complex ideas. They are an essential tool for writers, allowing them to convey subtle shades of meaning and tone. In the context of technical writing, em dashes can help to explain complex concepts, making them easier to understand for readers. For example:
```markdown
The new feature — which includes a range of tools and integrations — is designed to simplify the development process.
```
In this example, the em dash is used to set off a parenthetical phrase, providing additional information about the feature.
