## Introduction to QuadRF

QuadRF, a new technology developed by Jeff Geerling, has been making waves in the tech community for its ability to spot drones and see WiFi signals through walls. As reported on [Hacker News](https://www.jeffgeerling.com/blog/2026/quadrf-can-spot-drones-and-see-wifi-through-my-wall/), this technology has significant implications for developers, security experts, and the broader community. In this post, we'll delve into the details of QuadRF, explain why it matters to developers, and provide our take on the broader implications.

## How QuadRF Works

QuadRF uses advanced signal processing to detect and track drones, as well as identify WiFi signals emanating from devices behind walls. This is achieved through the use of specialized hardware and software that can analyze the unique radio frequency (RF) signatures of drones and WiFi devices. By analyzing these signatures, QuadRF can determine the location, speed, and direction of drones, as well as detect the presence of WiFi devices behind walls.
