## Introduction

In a recent announcement, OpenAI revealed its plans to acquire Ona, a company known for its expertise in building secure, persistent cloud environments. This acquisition is set to expand OpenAI's Codex, enabling long-running AI agents across enterprise workflows. As a developer, it's essential to understand the implications of this acquisition and how it may impact the future of AI development.

## What Happened

OpenAI's decision to acquire Ona stems from its desire to enhance its Codex capabilities. Codex is a cutting-edge AI model that can understand and generate code in various programming languages. By integrating Ona's technology, OpenAI aims to provide a more comprehensive and secure platform for developers to build and deploy AI-powered applications. The acquisition will enable OpenAI to offer secure, persistent cloud environments, allowing AI agents to run continuously and efficiently across enterprise workflows.
