## Introduction to Robotic Meal Prep

A recent article in [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/these-robots-are-making-meals-for-a-nonprofit-in-san-franciscos-tenderloin/) highlighted the use of robots in meal prep for a nonprofit in San Francisco's Tenderloin district. The nonprofit, which provides meals to those in need, has turned to robotic meal prep tech to make up for a dearth of human volunteers. This innovation is not only helping to feed those in need but also showcasing the potential of robots in the food industry.
