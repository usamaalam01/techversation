## Introduction to Premium Seats

OpenAI has recently announced the introduction of premium seats for ChatGPT Business, a move that is expected to significantly enhance the capabilities of businesses using the platform. By signing up before August 20, users can receive $100 in workspace credits and unlock higher usage limits for their team's most demanding work. This development is a significant step forward for businesses that rely on ChatGPT for their operations, and it has important implications for developers who work with the platform.

## What are Premium Seats?

Premium seats are essentially an upgraded version of the standard ChatGPT Business plan. They offer higher usage limits, which means that businesses can use the platform more extensively without hitting the limits that are imposed on standard plans. This is particularly useful for companies that have large teams or that need to perform complex tasks that require significant computational resources. With premium seats, businesses can scale their operations more easily and take full advantage of the capabilities of ChatGPT.
