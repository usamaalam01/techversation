## Introduction to SkyPilot

Hugging Face has announced the launch of SkyPilot, a platform that enables developers to run AI workloads on any cloud and store their data on Hugging Face with zero-egress fees. This move is significant, as it simplifies the process of deploying and managing AI models across different cloud providers. In this post, we'll delve into the details of SkyPilot, its implications for developers, and the broader impact on the AI and cloud computing landscape.

## What is SkyPilot?

SkyPilot is designed to provide a seamless experience for developers working with AI models. By allowing them to run workloads on any cloud, SkyPilot eliminates the need to worry about vendor lock-in or the complexities of managing multiple cloud providers. The zero-egress storage feature means that developers can store their data on Hugging Face without incurring additional fees for data transfer. This is particularly important for AI models, which often require large amounts of data to train and operate effectively.
