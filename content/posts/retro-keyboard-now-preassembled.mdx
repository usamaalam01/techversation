## What Happened

The Novelkeys Classic-TKL Underscore Edition is a wired keyboard that pays homage to the classic designs of the past. It features a tenkeyless (TKL) layout, which means it doesn't have a number pad. This design choice is intentional, as it allows for a more compact and efficient typing experience. The keyboard is designed with a retro aesthetic in mind, featuring a sleek and minimalist design that is sure to appeal to fans of vintage hardware.

The preassembled option is a significant development, as it caters to those who want a hassle-free retro typing experience without the need for wireless connectivity.
