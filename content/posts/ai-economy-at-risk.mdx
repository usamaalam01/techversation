## Introduction

The AI economy is facing significant challenges, and five industry experts recently shared their concerns at the Milken Global Conference in Beverly Hills. According to a report by [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/06/five-architects-of-the-ai-economy-explain-where-the-wheels-are-coming-off/), the experts discussed various issues, including chip shortages, orbital data centers, and the possibility that the underlying architecture of the tech is flawed.

## Challenges Facing the AI Economy

The experts highlighted several challenges that are hindering the growth and development of the AI economy. One of the primary concerns is the shortage of chips, which is essential for building and training AI models. This shortage is not only affecting the development of new AI technologies but also limiting the scalability of existing ones.

Another challenge is the lack of standardization in the AI industry. With different companies developing their own AI architectures and frameworks, it is becoming increasingly difficult to integrate and collaborate on projects. This lack of standardization is also making it challenging to develop and deploy AI models that can work seamlessly across different platforms and devices.

## Why it Matters to Developers

The challenges facing the AI economy have significant implications for developers. As the demand for AI technologies continues to grow, developers need to be aware of the potential roadblocks and limitations. For instance, the chip shortage may require developers to optimize their code and models to run on less powerful hardware, which can be a significant challenge.

Moreover, the lack of standardization in the AI industry means that developers need to be proficient in multiple frameworks and architectures, which can be time-consuming and costly. To address this challenge, developers can focus on developing skills that are transferable across different platforms and frameworks, such as data preprocessing, model training, and deployment.
