## Introduction to the Issue

A recent post on [Hacker News](https://nelson.cloud/ai-generated-images-discourage-me-from-reading-your-blog/) highlights an interesting phenomenon: AI-generated images can actually discourage readers from engaging with blog content. The author argues that the use of AI images can make blogs appear less authentic and less engaging, ultimately leading to a decline in readership. This trend has significant implications for bloggers and content creators who rely on AI-generated images to enhance their content.

## Why It Matters to Developers

As developers, we are often at the forefront of adopting new technologies, including AI-generated images. However, it's essential to consider the potential impact of these technologies on our audience. If AI-generated images are perceived as less authentic or less engaging, it could negatively affect the overall user experience of our applications or websites. For instance, a blog that uses AI-generated images to illustrate technical concepts may find that readers are less likely to engage with the content or share it with others.
