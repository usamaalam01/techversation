## Introduction

The recent release of Microsoft's new Outlook has been met with criticism from users and developers alike. According to a report on [Windows Latest](https://www.windowslatest.com/2026/06/15/microsofts-new-outlook-takes-10-seconds-to-do-what-outlook-classic-does-instantly-on-windows/), the new Outlook takes a whopping 10 seconds to perform tasks that Outlook Classic can do instantly. This significant performance difference has sparked a heated debate about the pros and cons of replacing legacy software with new technology.

## What Happened

The issue was first reported on [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/), where users shared their experiences with the new Outlook. Many reported that simple tasks, such as switching between folders or loading emails, took significantly longer than they did in Outlook Classic. Microsoft has not yet commented on the issue, but the company has been working to improve the performance of its software in recent years.

## Why it Matters to Developers

For developers, the controversy surrounding the new Outlook highlights the challenges of replacing legacy software with new technology. On one hand, new software can bring new features and improved security. On the other hand, it can also introduce performance issues and compatibility problems. As developers, it's essential to consider the potential impact of new technology on our users and to carefully test and optimize our software before releasing it to the public.

```python
import time

    time.sleep(10)

    print("Task completed")

start_time = time.time()

start_time = time.time()
```
