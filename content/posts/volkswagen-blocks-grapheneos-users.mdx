## What's Happening

GrapheneOS is a security-focused, open-source operating system that is designed to provide a more private and secure mobile experience. The OS is based on the Android Open Source Project (AOSP) and is popular among developers and power users who value security and customization. However, it seems that Volkswagen's app is no longer compatible with GrapheneOS, and users who try to access the app are being blocked.

According to reports on the GrapheneOS forums, the block is due to Volkswagen's security concerns. The company apparently believes that GrapheneOS is not secure enough to meet its standards, and therefore, it's blocking users from accessing the app. This move has been met with criticism from the GrapheneOS community, who argue that the decision is unfair and discriminatory.
