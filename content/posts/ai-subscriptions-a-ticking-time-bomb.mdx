## The Risks of AI Subscriptions
AI subscriptions can be a ticking time bomb for enterprises due to their unpredictable nature. Unlike traditional software subscriptions, AI subscriptions often involve complex algorithms and machine learning models that can change rapidly. This unpredictability can lead to sudden changes in the subscription's terms, pricing, or even the underlying technology. As a result, enterprises may find themselves facing unexpected costs, technical challenges, or even reputational damage.
