## Understanding Responsible Non-Compliance

Responsible non-compliance refers to the ability of an autonomous agent to deliberately not comply with a user request, while ensuring that its actions are still aligned with its overall goals and constraints. This concept is essential in scenarios where the agent's compliance with a user request could lead to undesirable outcomes, such as harm to humans or damage to the environment.

For example, consider a self-driving car that is instructed to drive at a speed of 100 km/h in a school zone. A responsibly non-compliant agent would recognize the potential danger of such an action and instead choose to comply with the traffic laws and regulations, even if it means not following the user's request.
