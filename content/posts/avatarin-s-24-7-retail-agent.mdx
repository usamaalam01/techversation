## Introduction

The retail industry has undergone significant transformations in recent years, with the rise of e-commerce and digital payments. However, one aspect that has remained largely unchanged is customer support. Traditional customer support models often rely on human representatives, who may not be available 24/7 or may not be able to provide support in multiple languages. This is where AI-powered solutions come in, and avatarin's recent development is a prime example. By leveraging OpenAI's GPT-Realtime, avatarin has built a 24/7 retail agent that provides multilingual support to Yamada Denki shoppers.

## What Happened

According to the [case study](https://openai.com/index/avatarin) published by OpenAI, avatarin used GPT-Realtime to build a retail agent that can provide support to customers in multiple languages. The agent was deployed in two weeks, and in that short span, it was used by 30,000 people. The results were impressive, with 92% of survey responses being positive. This is a significant achievement, considering the complexity of building an AI-powered customer support system that can understand and respond to customer queries in multiple languages.

The development of avatarin's retail agent has significant implications for developers, particularly those working in the retail and AI industries. Additionally, the multilingual capabilities of the agent can help retailers expand their customer base, catering to customers who may not speak the dominant language of the region.

```python
# Example code snippet demonstrating the use of GPT-Realtime
import openai

# Initialize the GPT-Realtime model
model = openai.Model("gpt-realtime")

# Define a function to generate responses to customer queries
def generate_response(query):
    response = model.generate(query)
    return response

# Test the function with a sample query
query = "What are the operating hours of your store?"
response = generate_response(query)
print(response)
```
