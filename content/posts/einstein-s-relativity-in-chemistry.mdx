## Why Relativity Matters in Chemistry

So, why does relativity matter in chemistry? To understand this, let's take a step back and look at the basics of chemical bonding. Chemical bonds are formed when atoms share or exchange electrons, resulting in a stable arrangement of atoms. However, as atoms get heavier, their electrons move faster and faster, approaching significant fractions of the speed of light. At these speeds, relativistic effects become important, and the traditional rules of chemistry no longer apply.

