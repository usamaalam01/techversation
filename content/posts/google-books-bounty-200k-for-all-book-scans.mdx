## Introduction to Google Books Bounty

Google Books, a massive book digitization project, has recently made headlines with its announcement of a [$200k bounty for all book scans in 2025](https://software.annas-archive.gl/AnnaArchivist/annas-archive/-/work_items/234). ## What Happened

The Google Books project was launched in 2004 with the aim of digitizing all books in the world. The project has made tremendous progress over the years, with millions of books already digitized and made available online. However, the project has faced several challenges, including copyright issues, scanning quality, and accessibility. The $200k bounty is an attempt to address these challenges and improve the overall quality of the book scans.
