## Introduction

A recent article on [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/) and a blog post by [Tom Bedor](https://tombedor.dev/human-attention-and-human-effort/) have sparked a lively discussion among developers about the importance of demonstrating human effort to get human attention in the tech industry. The idea is simple: if you want to capture someone's attention, you need to show that you've put in the effort to understand their needs and tailor your approach accordingly. In this post, we'll explore why this concept is resonating with developers and what it means for the future of tech.

## The Problem with Automated Outreach

In today's digital age, it's easier than ever to send automated messages or create generic content that can be blasted out to a large audience. However, this approach often falls flat, as people can tell when they're being targeted by a machine rather than a human. As the article points out, "if you are asking for human attention, demonstrate human effort." This means taking the time to research the person or organization you're trying to reach, understanding their specific needs and pain points, and crafting a personalized message that speaks to those needs.

## Why Human Effort Matters

So why is human effort so important in getting human attention? The answer lies in the way people process information and make decisions. When someone takes the time to understand our needs and tailor their approach accordingly, we're more likely to trust them and engage with their message. This is because we feel seen and heard, and we appreciate the effort they've put into understanding us. On the other hand, automated or generic messages can come across as lazy or insincere, and are often ignored or dismissed.

## Implications for Developers

So what does this mean for developers? In short, it means that we need to be more thoughtful and intentional in our outreach and communication efforts. Rather than relying on automated tools or generic messages, we need to take the time to research our audience, understand their needs, and craft personalized messages that speak to those needs. This might involve using more manual processes, such as email or phone calls, rather than relying on automated systems. It might also involve being more selective about who we reach out to, and taking the time to build relationships with key stakeholders.

## Broader Implications

The idea that human effort matters in getting human attention has broader implications for the tech industry as a whole. As we become increasingly reliant on automated systems and AI-powered tools, there's a risk that we'll lose sight of the importance of human connection and relationships. However, by prioritizing human effort and personalized communication, we can build stronger, more meaningful relationships with our users, customers, and colleagues. This, in turn, can lead to greater trust, loyalty, and engagement, and can help us to create more effective and sustainable solutions.

## Conclusion

In conclusion, the idea that human effort matters in getting human attention is a powerful one that has significant implications for developers and the tech industry as a whole. By prioritizing human effort and personalized communication, we can build stronger, more meaningful relationships with our users, customers, and colleagues, and create more effective and sustainable solutions. As the tech community continues to evolve, it's essential that we remember the importance of human connection and relationships, and strive to create solutions that are both innovative and humane.
