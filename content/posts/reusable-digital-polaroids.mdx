## Introduction to Reusable Digital Polaroids

A recent article from [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/917067/vidabay-snap-nfc-e-ink-paper-fridge-magnet-photo-hands-on) highlights a new product that caught my attention: reusable digital Polaroids. These small devices use NFC and e-ink technology to display memories on a fridge, combining the nostalgia of traditional Polaroids with the convenience of modern technology. 

As a developer, I'm intrigued by the potential applications of this technology. The use of NFC and e-ink displays suggests a low-power, low-maintenance device that can be easily integrated into various products. 

## How it Works

The reusable digital Polaroids work by using an app to send images to the device via NFC. The device then displays the image on its e-ink screen. This process can be repeated multiple times, allowing users to update the images on their fridge without having to print out new physical copies. 

The technology behind these devices is not new, but the application is innovative. E-ink displays have been used in e-readers and other devices for years, but combining them with NFC and a user-friendly app creates a unique product.
