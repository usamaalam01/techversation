## Introduction to Startup Battlefield 200
The deadline for Startup Battlefield 200 applications is rapidly approaching, with only three days left to submit. As of June 8, 11:59 p.m. PT, the window for applying to this prestigious competition will officially close. For startup founders and entrepreneurs, this is a crucial moment, as securing a spot in Startup Battlefield 200 can be a significant catalyst for their business.

## Why It Matters to Developers
So, why should developers care about Startup Battlefield 200? The answer lies in the potential for exposure and the opportunity to launch their product or service on a global stage. TechCrunch Disrupt 2026, where the competition will take place, is one of the most prominent tech conferences in the world, attracting investors, media, and industry leaders. By participating in Startup Battlefield 200, startups can gain valuable feedback, networking opportunities, and press coverage, all of which can be instrumental in driving growth and attracting funding.

### The Competition
The competition itself is fierce, with hundreds of startups vying for a limited number of spots. To be considered, applicants must demonstrate a strong, innovative product or service, a clear understanding of their market and target audience, and a compelling vision for growth. The selection process is rigorous, with a panel of expert judges reviewing each application to identify the most promising startups.

## What Comes Next
For those who secure a spot in Startup Battlefield 200, the real work begins. Participants will have the opportunity to refine their pitches, receive mentorship and feedback from industry experts, and prepare for the main event at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026. The conference, which will take place in October at San Francisco's Moscone West, promises to be an exciting and intense experience, with startups competing for a $100,000 equity-free prize and the coveted Disrupt Cup.
