## Introduction

Lenovo has recently announced the next generation of its Tab Plus tablet, featuring a thick speaker bump and an upgraded kickstand. The new device, dubbed Tab Plus Gen 2, boasts 9 built-in speakers, a significant upgrade from its predecessor's 8 speakers. This move showcases Lenovo's commitment to delivering high-quality audio in its tablets, making them an attractive option for entertainment and multimedia use.

## What Happened

According to a report by [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/949617/lenovo-tab-plus-gen-2-tablet-9-speakers-bluetooth-android-kickstand), the original Tab Plus launched two years ago with a 13.58mm rear bulge to accommodate its 8 speakers. The new Tab Plus Gen 2 takes this concept further, adding an extra speaker to the mix. The device's focus on audio quality is evident in its design, with the speaker bump being a prominent feature.

```java
// Example of how to access and utilize the device's speakers in Android
import android.media.AudioManager;
import android.media.SoundPool;

public class SpeakerExample {
    private SoundPool soundPool;
    private int soundId;

    public SpeakerExample() {
        soundPool = new SoundPool(1, AudioManager.STREAM_MUSIC, 0);
        soundId = soundPool.load(this, R.raw.example_sound, 1);
    }

    public void playSound() {
```
