## Introduction
In a surprise move, IMAX announced that Spider-Man: Brand New Day will be available in select digital IMAX locations across North America, joining The Odyssey which had an exclusive three-weekend run. This decision comes after a record-breaking weekend at the box office, with both movies contributing to the biggest weekend in box office history. As reported by [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/entertainment/974411/spider-man-the-odyssey-imax), this move marks a significant shift in the way cinematic experiences are distributed and exhibited.
