## Introduction to the TI-84 Evo

The TI-84 Evo, recently announced by Texas Instruments, marks a significant update to the classic TI-84 graphing calculator. This new device is designed to provide students with a more efficient and effective way to learn math and science concepts. As reported on [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/), the TI-84 Evo has generated considerable interest among educators and developers.

## What's New in the TI-84 Evo

The TI-84 Evo features several improvements over its predecessors, including enhanced processing power, increased memory, and a revamped user interface. These updates enable faster calculation and graphing capabilities, making it an attractive tool for students and educators alike. According to the [official Texas Instruments website](https://education.ti.com/en/products/calculators/graphing-calculators/ti-84-evo), the TI-84 Evo also includes new features such as improved algebra and calculus functionality, as well as enhanced statistics and data analysis capabilities.

### Key Features and Specifications

Some of the key features and specifications of the TI-84 Evo include:
* High-resolution color display
* Enhanced algebra and calculus functionality
* Improved statistics and data analysis capabilities
* Increased memory and processing power
* Compatibility with existing TI-84 software and accessories
