## The Story

The article cites a study by Dr. Ken Murray, a physician who observed that doctors often choose to forgo aggressive medical treatments when faced with terminal illnesses. Instead, they opt for a more minimalist approach, focusing on comfort and quality of life. This contrasts with the general population, who often undergo extensive medical interventions in their final days. The study suggests that doctors, having witnessed the consequences of aggressive treatments, are more inclined to prioritize a peaceful death over prolonged suffering.
