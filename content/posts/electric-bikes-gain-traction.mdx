## Introduction to Electric Mountain Bikes

A recent article from The Verge, titled [Fine, electric mountain bikes don’t suck](https://www.theverge.com/tech/966964/emtb-riders-are-not-cheaters), has sparked a conversation about the growing acceptance of electric mountain bikes (e-bikes) among cycling enthusiasts. The author's change of heart towards e-bikes, after riding an Amflow PX, highlights the fun and accessibility that these bikes offer. This shift in perception is not only relevant to the cycling community but also has broader implications for the tech industry and innovation.

## Why Electric Bikes Matter to Developers

As electric bikes gain popularity, there is a growing need for innovative technologies to support their development and integration. This includes advancements in battery technology, motor design, and user interface systems. Developers can play a crucial role in shaping the future of e-bikes by creating software and hardware solutions that enhance the riding experience. For example, mobile apps can be designed to track ride metrics, provide navigation, and offer maintenance alerts. 

```python
import numpy as np

# Sample code for tracking ride metrics
class RideTracker:
    def __init__(self):
        self.distance = 0
        self.speed = 0

    def update_metrics(self, new_distance, new_speed):
        self.distance = new_distance
        self.speed = new_speed

    def print_metrics(self):
        print(f"Distance: {self.distance} km")
        print(f"Speed: {self.speed} km/h")

# Create an instance of RideTracker
tracker = RideTracker()

# Update and print ride metrics
tracker.update_metrics(10, 20)
tracker.print_metrics()
```

Moreover, the technology developed for e-bikes can have spin-off benefits in other areas, such as electric vehicles and renewable energy systems.
