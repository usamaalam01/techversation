## Introduction to Ad Testing in ChatGPT

OpenAI has announced that it is testing ads in ChatGPT, a move aimed at supporting free access to the popular AI chatbot. According to the [official announcement](https://openai.com/index/testing-ads-in-chatgpt), the ads will be clearly labeled, and the company has emphasized its commitment to maintaining answer independence and strong privacy protections. This development has significant implications for developers, users, and the broader AI community.
