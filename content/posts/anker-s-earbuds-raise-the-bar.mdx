## Introduction to Anker's New Earbuds

Anker, a well-known brand in the tech industry, has made a significant leap forward with its new Liberty 5 Pro earbuds. According to a review by [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/935766/soundcore-liberty-5-pro-series-earbuds-review), these earbuds have the best call quality the reviewer has ever heard. This is a notable achievement, especially considering Anker's audio brand, Soundcore, has primarily focused on budget-to-midrange products.

## Why It Matters to Developers

The improved call quality in Anker's new earbuds is largely due to the company's new Thus chip, which has more processing power than previous Soundcore earbuds. This increase in processing power allows for more advanced noise cancellation and audio processing techniques, resulting in clearer and more reliable call quality. For developers working on audio-related projects, this is significant because it raises the bar for what is possible in terms of audio quality in earbuds.
