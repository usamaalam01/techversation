## Introduction

A recent ruling by a German court has sent shockwaves through the tech industry, as it holds Google liable for false statements generated by AI overviews. According to a report by [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/a-court-has-ruled-that-google-is-liable-for-false-statements-generated-by-ai-overviews/), the court's decision marks a significant shift in the way companies are held responsible for the output of their AI systems.

## What Happened

The court ruled that a company that designs, trains, operates, and manages an AI system must assume legal liability for any damages caused by the responses it generates. This means that Google, and potentially other companies, can be held accountable for false or misleading information produced by their AI systems. The ruling is a result of a lawsuit filed against Google, alleging that the company's AI-generated overviews contained false information.
