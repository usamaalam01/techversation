## Introduction to NotebookLM's Update

In a significant move, Google has announced that its NotebookLM platform will now utilize Gemini 3.5 as its default model. This update, as reported by [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/08/notebooklms-new-update-will-help-you-build-source-repository-from-chat/), enables developers to build source repositories directly from chat interactions. This capability promises to revolutionize the way developers work, especially in collaborative and AI-assisted development environments.

## What Happened

The integration of Gemini 3.5 into NotebookLM is a substantial enhancement. Gemini 3.5 is known for its advanced language understanding and generation capabilities, making it an ideal choice for tasks that require complex interactions, such as building source repositories from chat inputs. By making this model the default, Google is essentially providing developers with a powerful tool to streamline their workflow. The update means that developers can now engage in natural language conversations with NotebookLM, and the platform can interpret these conversations to generate code and manage source repositories accordingly.

### Technical Implications

From a technical standpoint, this update signifies a major leap forward in AI-assisted development. The ability to generate and manage code directly from chat inputs simplifies the development process, reducing the need for manual coding and version control. This can lead to faster development cycles and reduced errors, as the AI model can automatically handle routine tasks and ensure consistency across the codebase.
