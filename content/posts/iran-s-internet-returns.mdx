## Introduction to the Blackout
After nearly 90 days of internet blackout, Iran has started to see the return of online connectivity, according to web monitoring groups. This development marks a significant shift in the country's digital landscape, which was severely restricted in response to nationwide protests. As reported by [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/internet-in-iran-starts-to-return-after-3-month-blackout/), the restoration of internet services, although uncertain in its permanence, holds considerable importance for developers, Iranian citizens, and the global discussion on internet freedom.

## Impact on Developers and the Global Community
The blackout in Iran not only affected the local population but also had implications for the global developer community. Many Iranian developers contribute to open-source projects and collaborate with international teams. The lack of internet connectivity hindered their ability to participate in these projects, potentially slowing down the development of critical software and technologies. With the internet starting to return, these developers can once again engage with the global community, share their expertise, and benefit from international collaborations.

### Open-Source Contributions
For instance, Iranian developers are known to contribute to projects like [Linux](https://www.linux.org/), which relies heavily on global contributions. The absence of these contributors due to the blackout could have long-term effects on the project's development and security. Now, as connectivity resumes, their return to the development scene can enhance the project's diversity and robustness.

## Broader Implications for Internet Freedom
The temporary but significant blackout in Iran underscores the fragile nature of internet freedom worldwide. Governments' ability to control and restrict internet access poses a threat to global digital rights and freedoms. This incident serves as a reminder of the importance of advocating for an open and accessible internet, where information can flow freely without fear of censorship or retribution.

### Technological Solutions for Internet Freedom
In response to such blackouts, developers have been working on technologies that can help circumvent restrictions and ensure continued access to the internet. Tools like [VPN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virtual_private_network) services and [Tor](https://www.torproject.org/) have been crucial for many in bypassing government-imposed blocks. However, the development and deployment of these technologies are ongoing challenges, requiring continuous innovation and support from the developer community.
