## Introduction to AI Psychosis

The term "AI psychosis" has been circulating in the tech community, particularly on platforms like [Hacker News](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/27/tech-ceos-are-apparently-suffering-from-ai-psychosis/), to describe the growing concern among tech CEOs about the integration of artificial intelligence into their businesses. This phenomenon is not just about the fear of job displacement or the ethics of AI development but also about the psychological impact of trying to keep up with the rapid advancements in AI technology.

## Why It Matters to Developers

As developers, we are at the forefront of AI integration. We are responsible for designing, implementing, and maintaining AI systems that can have a significant impact on businesses and society. The rise of AI psychosis among tech CEOs is a signal that we need to be more aware of the potential risks and challenges associated with AI development. It is essential to consider the human factor in AI development, including the potential for burnout, anxiety, and decreased productivity among developers and CEOs alike.
