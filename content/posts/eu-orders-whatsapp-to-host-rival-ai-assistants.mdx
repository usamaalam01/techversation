## Introduction to the EU's Decision

In a significant move, the European Commission has ordered Meta to restore free WhatsApp access for chatbots made by rival AI providers. This decision is part of the EU's ongoing antitrust investigation into Meta, aiming to prevent damage to competition in the general-purpose AI assistant market. As reported by [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/947516/meta-whatsapp-eu-third-party-ai-chatbot-ban-order), this rare interim measure is deemed necessary to ensure a level playing field for all AI assistant providers.

This move can lead to increased innovation in the AI assistant market, as developers from rival companies can now integrate their services with WhatsApp, one of the most widely used messaging platforms.
