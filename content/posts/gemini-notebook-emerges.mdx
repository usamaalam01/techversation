## What Happened?

NotebookLM, a popular AI model, has been rebranded as Gemini Notebook. This change is not just a superficial one; it reflects a fundamental shift in the way AI models are developed and interacted with. According to the [official blog post](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-notebook/notebooklm-gemini-notebook/), Gemini Notebook is designed to provide a more seamless and intuitive experience for developers working with AI models.
