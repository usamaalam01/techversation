## What are mRNA Vaccines?

mRNA (messenger RNA) vaccines use a piece of genetic material called messenger RNA to instruct cells in the body to produce a specific protein. This protein triggers an immune response, which helps to fight against diseases. The technology has been gaining attention in recent years due to its potential to rapidly develop vaccines against emerging diseases.
