### Key Terms and Concepts

The TechCrunch AI glossary covers a wide range of topics, including basic definitions, machine learning, deep learning, and natural language processing. Some of the key terms and concepts included in the glossary are:

* **Hallucinations**: a phenomenon where an AI model generates output that is not based on any actual input or data.
* **Deep learning**: a type of machine learning that uses neural networks to analyze and interpret data.
* **Natural language processing**: a field of study focused on the interaction between computers and human language.
