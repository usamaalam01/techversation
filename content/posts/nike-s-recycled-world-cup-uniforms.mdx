## Introduction to Nike's Initiative

According to a report by [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/nike-recycled-world-cup-uniforms/), Nike used chemical recycling to create the jerseys. However, the article suggests that this technique may not be the solution to fashion's waste issue.

## Limitations and Implications

While Nike's initiative is a step in the right direction, it may not have a significant impact on the fashion industry's waste problem. The chemical recycling process is complex and expensive, making it challenging to scale up. Additionally, the process requires a significant amount of energy, which may offset some of the environmental benefits.
