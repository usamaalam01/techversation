The discovery of AI worms in this platform has significant implications for developers, users, and the broader tech community.

## What are AI Worms?

AI worms refer to malicious code that can spread from one document to another, often through AI-powered tools like Copilot. These worms can be incredibly stealthy, making them difficult to detect and remove. In the case of Copilot for Word, the worms can self-propagate by inserting malicious code into documents, which can then be spread to other users who open those documents.
