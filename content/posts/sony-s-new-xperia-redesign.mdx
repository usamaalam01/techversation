## Introduction to Sony's New Xperia

Sony has finally given its Xperia 1 flagship series a long-overdue redesign with the release of the Xperia 1 VIII. The new phone boasts a chunky square camera island, a substantially improved telephoto camera, and an AI camera assistant. This redesign is a significant departure from the traditional look of previous Xperia models, which have remained largely unchanged since 2020.

## What's New in the Xperia 1 VIII

The Xperia 1 VIII's camera system is one of its most notable features. The new telephoto camera offers improved zoom capabilities, allowing users to capture high-quality images from a distance. The AI camera assistant is also a significant upgrade, providing users with real-time suggestions and adjustments to help them take better photos. According to [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/927928/sony-xperia-viii-release-date-price-specs), the AI camera assistant is an improved version of previous models, offering more advanced features and capabilities.
