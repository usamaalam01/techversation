The European Union has announced plans to crack down on TikTok and Instagram over their use of "addictive design" targeting kids. This move has sent shockwaves through the tech industry, with many wondering what this means for developers and the future of social media. ## What Happened

According to a report by CNBC, the EU is targeting TikTok and Instagram for their alleged use of addictive design techniques, such as infinite scrolling and push notifications, to keep kids engaged on their platforms. The EU claims that these techniques are designed to manipulate users, particularly children, into spending more time on the apps, which can have negative effects on their mental and physical health.
