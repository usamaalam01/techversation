## Introduction to Keurig's Revolution

Before Keurig, office coffee was often a disappointing experience. The coffee was usually old, burned, and made without much care. However, with the introduction of Keurig's single-cup brewer, the game changed. As [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/podcast/961470/keurig-coffee-k-up-version-history) notes, Keurig's innovative solution allowed individuals to make their own coffee, one cup at a time, exactly when they needed it.

### The Environmental Impact

The environmental impact of Keurig's single-cup brewer is a significant concern. The use of disposable K-cups has led to a massive amount of waste, with millions of cups ending up in landfills every year. This issue serves as a reminder for developers to consider the environmental implications of their products and to design solutions that minimize waste and promote sustainability.
