## Introduction to Built-In Travel eSIM

In a recent development, Motorola has announced a partnership with Gigs to provide a built-in travel eSIM for mobile data outside the US. This feature is available in select markets and aims to simplify the process of staying connected while traveling abroad. As reported by [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/exclusive-motorola-phones-now-get-a-built-in-travel-esim/), this collaboration marks a significant step forward in making international travel more convenient for Motorola phone owners.
