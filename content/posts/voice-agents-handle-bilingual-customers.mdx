## Introduction to Voice Agents and Bilingual Customers

The ability of voice agents to handle bilingual customers is a crucial aspect of providing effective customer support. With the increasing use of voice assistants and chatbots, it's essential to ensure that these systems can understand and respond to customers who speak multiple languages. Recently, HuggingFace [published a blog post](https://huggingface.co/blog/ServiceNow-AI/code-switching) on benchmarking Frontier ASR on code-switched speech, which has significant implications for developers and the future of voice agents.

## What is Code-Switched Speech?

Code-switched speech refers to the practice of switching between two or more languages within a single conversation. This phenomenon is common in bilingual communities, where individuals may switch between languages to convey meaning, express identity, or accommodate their conversational partner. However, code-switched speech poses a significant challenge for automatic speech recognition (ASR) systems, which are typically trained on monolingual data.

## Benchmarking Frontier ASR on Code-Switched Speech

The HuggingFace team benchmarked Frontier ASR on a dataset of code-switched speech to evaluate its performance on bilingual customer support. The results show that Frontier ASR achieves promising performance on code-switched speech, with a significant improvement over baseline models. This is a notable achievement, as it demonstrates the potential of ASR systems to handle bilingual customers effectively.
