## Introduction to the Problem
A recent survey revealed that sixty percent of US consumers say the term 'AI' in brand messaging is a turnoff. This finding, reported on [Hacker News](https://wpvip.com/future-of-the-web-2026/), highlights a growing concern among marketers and developers about the perception of AI-powered technologies. The survey's results suggest that consumers are becoming increasingly skeptical of companies that use the term 'AI' as a marketing gimmick, rather than a genuine technological innovation.

## Why It Matters to Developers
For developers, this trend matters because it underscores the need to focus on creating meaningful and transparent AI-powered solutions. Rather than relying on the term 'AI' as a marketing buzzword, developers must prioritize building technologies that provide real value to users. This shift in focus will help to rebuild trust among consumers and demonstrate the genuine potential of AI to improve their lives.

### The Importance of Transparency
To address the growing distrust of AI, developers must prioritize transparency in their design and marketing strategies. This can involve clearly explaining how AI-powered technologies work, as well as providing users with control over their data and interactions with AI systems. By being open and honest about the capabilities and limitations of AI, developers can help to educate consumers and build trust in their products.

## Broader Implications
The survey's findings have broader implications for the tech industry as a whole. As companies continue to invest in AI research and development, they must also consider the potential backlash against AI-powered technologies. To mitigate this risk, companies can focus on creating AI-powered solutions that are intuitive, user-friendly, and transparent. For example, a company like Coursera, which offers online courses and degree programs, can use AI to personalize the learning experience for its users, while also providing clear explanations of how the technology works.
