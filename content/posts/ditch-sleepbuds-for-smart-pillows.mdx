## Introduction to the Sleepbud Alternative

The tech industry is known for its innovative solutions to everyday problems. However, these solutions often come with a hefty price tag. Sleepbuds, miniature earbuds designed for sleeping, are a prime example of this trend. Companies like Anker and Ozlo have been selling these devices, which allow side-sleepers to listen to podcasts, audiobooks, music, or white noise without disturbing others. Recently, a new alternative has emerged in the form of a smart pillow, which could potentially disrupt the sleepbud market.

## The Rise of Smart Pillows

The concept of smart pillows is not new, but the latest iteration has gained significant attention due to its cost-effectiveness and practicality. According to an article by [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/970473/forget-expensive-sleepbuds-buy-this-pillow-instead), this smart pillow offers a more sensible solution to the problems addressed by sleepbuds. The pillow is designed to provide a comfortable sleeping experience while also offering features like white noise and audio playback.
