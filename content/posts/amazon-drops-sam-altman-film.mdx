## Introduction to the Story
The film about Sam Altman, CEO of OpenAI, has been dropped by Amazon MGM, as reported by [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/952906/sam-altman-film-artificial-openai-amazon-mgm-dropped). The film, titled Artificial, was set to star Andrew Garfield and cover the five-day period in 2023 when Altman was terminated and then reinstated as CEO. This project had been in the works for about a year before being dropped.
