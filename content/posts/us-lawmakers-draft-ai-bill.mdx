## Introduction
The US House of Representatives has released a draft bill to regulate AI technology, aiming to provide a federal framework for AI development and prohibit state AI rules. This move is seen as a significant step towards establishing a unified approach to AI regulation in the United States. As a developer, it's essential to understand the implications of this bill and how it may affect your work with AI technology.

## Background
The draft bill is a response to the growing concern about the lack of regulation in the AI industry. With AI technology becoming increasingly prevalent in various aspects of life, there is a need for a comprehensive framework to ensure its safe and responsible development. The bill aims to address this need by providing a federal framework for AI regulation, which would supersede state-level regulations.

## Overview of the Bill
The draft bill proposes to establish a federal agency responsible for overseeing AI development and regulation. The agency would be tasked with creating guidelines for AI development, ensuring compliance with federal regulations, and providing support for AI research and development. The bill also proposes to prohibit state-level AI regulations, aiming to create a unified approach to AI regulation across the United States.
