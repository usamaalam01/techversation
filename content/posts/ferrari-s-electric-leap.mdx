## Introduction to the Ferrari Luce

In a recent article by WIRED, the publication had the opportunity to test-drive a preproduction version of the Ferrari Luce, the company's first electric car. The Luce has been a topic of discussion among car enthusiasts, with some praising its innovative design and others criticizing its departure from traditional Ferrari roots. The test drive revealed a thrilling drive experience, which may just convert Ferrari fanatics to the electric side.
