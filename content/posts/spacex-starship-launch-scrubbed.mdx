## Introduction to the Incident

On July 17, 2026, SpaceX scrubbed the launch of its Starship spacecraft due to technical issues with some of its engines. According to a report by [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/07/spacex-scrubs-starship-launch-after-some-of-its-engines-didnt-start/), the launch was aborted after some of the engines failed to start, prompting the team to offload propellant and prepare for a new launch attempt.
