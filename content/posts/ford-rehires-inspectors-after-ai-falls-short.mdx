## Introduction to the Story
Ford, the American automaker, has been rehiring human quality inspectors after its AI-powered inspection system failed to meet expectations. According to a report by [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-25/ford-has-been-rehiring-quality-inspectors-after-ai-fell-short), the company's AI system was unable to detect certain defects and anomalies in the manufacturing process, leading to a decrease in product quality.

## Why It Matters to Developers
The story is significant for developers because it highlights the limitations of AI in certain industries. While AI has made tremendous progress in recent years, it is not a replacement for human expertise in all cases. In the automotive industry, where safety and quality are paramount, human inspectors play a crucial role in ensuring that vehicles meet the required standards. The fact that Ford is rehiring human inspectors after investing in AI-powered inspection systems suggests that AI is not yet ready to fully replace human quality control.

### The Role of AI in Quality Control
AI can be useful in quality control, particularly in detecting patterns and anomalies in large datasets. However, when it comes to complex tasks that require human judgment and expertise, AI may not be sufficient. In the case of Ford, the AI system was unable to detect certain defects and anomalies that human inspectors could easily identify. This highlights the need for a hybrid approach that combines the strengths of AI with human expertise.
