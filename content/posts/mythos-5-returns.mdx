## Introduction to Mythos 5

Anthropic's Mythos 5, a cutting-edge AI model, has been at the center of a heated negotiation process with the Trump administration. After a rollercoaster two weeks, Mythos 5 is finally back in action, albeit for a select group of organizations. This development has significant implications for developers, the AI community, and the future of AI regulation.

## What Happened

According to a letter from the government to Anthropic, viewed by The Verge, Mythos 5 is back online for certain organizations. However, Fable 5, the public-facing Mythos-class model, remains inaccessible. The negotiations between Anthropic and the Trump administration were likely centered around the potential risks and benefits of Mythos 5, as well as the need for regulatory oversight.
