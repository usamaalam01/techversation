## What is Homomorphic Encryption?

Homomorphic encryption is a form of encryption that allows computations to be performed on encrypted data without decrypting it first. This means that sensitive information can be processed and analyzed while maintaining its confidentiality. The technique has been touted as a game-changer for privacy and security, especially in applications where data sharing is necessary but data protection is paramount.
