## Introduction to AI Notetakers

The way we take notes during meetings is about to change. Wispr Flow, a popular dictation tool, has released a live notetaker that can transcribe and summarize meetings in real-time. This innovation joins a growing wave of AI notetakers designed for the workplace, as reported by [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/ai-notetakers-invited-to-the-meetings/). 

## What Happened

Wispr Flow's AI notetaker uses natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning algorithms to capture and summarize the key points of a meeting. This technology has the potential to increase productivity by reducing the time spent on note-taking and allowing participants to focus on the discussion. The AI notetaker can also help to ensure that all important points are captured, even if the note-taker misses something.

### How it Works

The AI notetaker works by using speech recognition technology to transcribe the audio of the meeting. The transcription is then summarized using NLP algorithms, which identify the key points and action items. The summary is then made available to all participants, either in real-time or after the meeting.

For developers, the emergence of AI notetakers is significant because it demonstrates the increasing importance of NLP and machine learning in the workplace.
```python
import speech_recognition as sr
import nltk
from nltk.tokenize import word_tokenize

# Initialize speech recognition
r = sr.Recognizer()

# Use speech recognition to transcribe audio
with sr.AudioFile("meeting_audio.wav") as source:
    audio = r.record(source)
    try:
        transcription = r.recognize_google(audio)
        print(transcription)
    except sr.UnknownValueError:
        print("Could not understand audio")
    except sr.RequestError as e:
        print("Error requesting transcription: {0}".format(e))

# Tokenize the transcription
tokens = word_tokenize(transcription)
print(tokens)
```
