## Introduction
LG has recently announced the UltraGear 25G590B, the first gaming monitor to feature a native 1000Hz refresh rate at 1080p resolution. This is a significant improvement over previous models, which were only capable of 1000Hz at lower resolutions. In this tutorial, we'll take a closer look at the key features of the UltraGear 25G590B and provide a hands-on review of the monitor.

## Key Features
The UltraGear 25G590B boasts an impressive array of features, including:

* Native 1000Hz refresh rate at 1080p resolution
* 1ms response time
* NVIDIA G-Sync technology
* HDR10 support
* 25-inch IPS display

To take full advantage of these features, you'll need a powerful graphics card. We recommend using an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3080 or AMD Radeon RX 6800 XT.

### Optimizing Your Gaming Setup
To get the best experience from the UltraGear 25G590B, you'll need to optimize your gaming setup. This includes:

* Using a high-performance graphics card
* Adjusting your monitor settings for optimal performance
* Using a low-latency gaming mouse and keyboard

Here's an example of how you can adjust your monitor settings using the LG UltraGear software:
