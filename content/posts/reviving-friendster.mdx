## The Rise and Fall of Friendster
Launched in 2002, Friendster was one of the first social media platforms to gain widespread popularity. It allowed users to create profiles, connect with friends, and share content. At its peak, Friendster had over 100 million users and was valued at over $50 million. However, the platform's popularity declined with the rise of other social media sites like MySpace and Facebook. Despite attempts to revamp the site, Friendster was eventually sold to a Malaysian company, which used the platform to focus on the Asian market.
