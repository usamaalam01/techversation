## Introduction to the Problem
The US healthcare system has long been criticized for its high costs and poor outcomes. A recent study, as reported by [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/health/2026/05/us-healthcare-still-stupidly-expensive-with-pathetic-outcomes-study-finds/), has reaffirmed this notion, finding that the US healthcare system remains one of the most expensive, yet produces some of the poorest outcomes among developed countries. This is a complex issue with multiple factors at play, but one thing is clear: the US is not adopting the strategies that have been successful in other countries.

## The Role of Technology in Healthcare
Technology has the potential to revolutionize the healthcare industry, and it's already being used in various ways to improve patient outcomes and reduce costs. For instance, electronic health records (EHRs) can help reduce medical errors and improve patient care. Furthermore, wearable devices and mobile apps can help patients track their health and fitness, and provide valuable insights to healthcare providers.

```python
# Example of how EHRs can be used to improve patient care
class Patient:
    def __init__(self, name, medical_history):
        self.name = name
        self.medical_history = medical_history

    def get_medical_history(self):
        return self.medical_history

# Create a patient object
patient = Patient("John Doe", ["diabetes", "hypertension"])

# Get the patient's medical history
medical_history = patient.get_medical_history()
print(medical_history)
```
