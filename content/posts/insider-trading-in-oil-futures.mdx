## Introduction to Insider Trading in Oil Futures

The recent story of "Grand Theft Oil Futures" has been making waves in the financial community, with insider traders allegedly making a killing at the expense of the general public. According to an article by Paul Krugman on his Substack page, [https://paulkrugman.substack.com/p/grand-theft-oil-futures](https://paulkrugman.substack.com/p/grand-theft-oil-futures), this issue highlights the need for improved regulatory oversight and transparency in financial markets.

## What Happened

Insider trading in oil futures refers to the practice of using confidential information to trade oil futures contracts, giving certain individuals or groups an unfair advantage in the market. This can lead to artificial price fluctuations, affecting not only the oil industry but also the broader economy. The article suggests that insider traders are exploiting loopholes in the system, using advanced technology to stay one step ahead of regulators.

```python
import pandas as pd

# Sample data
data = {
    'trade_id': [1, 2, 3],
    'trade_time': ['2022-01-01 12:00:00', '2022-01-01 12:01:00', '2022-01-01 12:02:00'],
    'trade_price': [100.0, 101.0, 102.0]
}

# Create a pandas DataFrame
df = pd.DataFrame(data)

# Define a function to detect suspicious activity
def detect_suspicious_activity(df):
    # Calculate the average trade price
    avg_price = df['trade_price'].mean()
    
    # Identify trades with prices that deviate significantly from the average
    suspicious_trades = df[(df['trade_price'] > avg_price * 1.1) | (df['trade_price'] < avg_price * 0.9)]
    
    return suspicious_trades

# Detect suspicious activity
suspicious_trades = detect_suspicious_activity(df)

# Print the results
print(suspicious_trades)
```
This code example demonstrates a simple approach to detecting suspicious activity in trade data. By using data analysis and machine learning techniques, developers can build more sophisticated systems to prevent insider trading and promote fairness in financial markets.
