## What is AI Psychosis?

AI psychosis is a term used to describe the anxiety and fear that people experience when they think about the potential consequences of AI on their jobs and lives. This fear can be irrational, as it is often based on misconceptions about the capabilities and limitations of AI. However, it is a real concern that companies must address in order to create a positive and productive work environment.

### Causes of AI Psychosis

There are several causes of AI psychosis, including:

* Misinformation about AI: Many people have misconceptions about the capabilities and limitations of AI, which can lead to fear and anxiety.
* Job insecurity: The fear of being replaced by AI can lead to job insecurity and anxiety.
* Lack of understanding: Many people do not fully understand how AI works, which can lead to fear and mistrust.
