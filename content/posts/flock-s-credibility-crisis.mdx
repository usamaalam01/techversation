Flock Safety, a company that provides license plate reader technology, has been embroiled in a credibility crisis after being accused of repeatedly lying to city councils, police departments, and the public. ## What Happened
According to the ACLU, Flock Safety has made false claims about the effectiveness of its technology, the data it collects, and the ways in which it is used. The company has also been accused of downplaying the risks associated with its technology, including the potential for misuse and the impact on marginalized communities. These allegations have led to a credibility crisis for Flock Safety, with many city councils and police departments reevaluating their relationships with the company.
