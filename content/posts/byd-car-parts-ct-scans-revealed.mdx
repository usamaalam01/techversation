## What are CT Scans?
CT (Computed Tomography) scans are a type of non-destructive testing that uses X-rays to create detailed images of an object's internal structure. In the context of car parts, CT scans can be used to inspect the internal components of a vehicle without disassembling it. This technology has numerous applications in the automotive industry, including quality control, reverse engineering, and research and development.
