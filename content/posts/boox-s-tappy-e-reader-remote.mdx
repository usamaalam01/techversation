## Introduction to the Tappy

Boox, a company known for its e-readers and tablets, has recently announced the release of the Tappy, a small Bluetooth remote that allows users to control their devices with ease. The Tappy is a two-button keyboard that can be used to turn pages, scroll through content, and even control music playback. This new device is a significant development in the world of e-readers, and it has important implications for developers.

## Why the Tappy Matters

The Tappy is more than just a simple page-turning device. It offers a range of features that make it a valuable accessory for anyone who uses an e-reader or tablet. For example, the Tappy can be used to scroll through vertical content in browsers or social media apps, making it easier to navigate through long articles or posts. It can also be used to skip to the next or previous track when listening to music, allowing users to control their music playback without having to physically interact with their device.
