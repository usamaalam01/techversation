## Introduction to the Update

Amazon has quietly updated its 2023 Fire HD 10 tablet, increasing the RAM from 3GB to 4GB. This change is significant, as it enhances the device's performance and user experience. According to a report by [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/961387/amazon-2023-fire-hd-10-tablet-4gb-update), the update is specifically for the 32GB version of the tablet.

## Why it Matters to Developers

The update matters to developers for several reasons. Firstly, the increased RAM allows for smoother app performance and faster multitasking. This is particularly important for developers who create resource-intensive apps, as it ensures that their apps can run efficiently on the device. Secondly, the update demonstrates Amazon's commitment to improving its budget tablet lineup, which is popular among developers who use these devices for testing and debugging.
