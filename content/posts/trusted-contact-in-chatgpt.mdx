## Introduction to Trusted Contact

OpenAI has recently introduced Trusted Contact, an optional safety feature in ChatGPT that notifies a trusted individual if the AI model detects serious self-harm concerns during a conversation. This feature is a significant step towards prioritizing user safety and well-being in AI interactions. As developers, it's essential to understand the implications of this feature and how it may impact the future of AI development.

## How Trusted Contact Works

Trusted Contact is designed to be an optional feature, allowing users to nominate a trusted individual who will be notified if ChatGPT detects potential self-harm concerns. This feature uses advanced AI algorithms to identify potential risks and alert the nominated contact. The introduction of Trusted Contact demonstrates OpenAI's commitment to creating a safer and more responsible AI environment.
