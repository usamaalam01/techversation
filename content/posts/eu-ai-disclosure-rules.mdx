## Introduction to EU AI Disclosure Rules

The European Union has recently introduced new rules that will significantly impact how companies operate in the region. As reported by [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/europeans-are-about-to-find-out-how-entrenched-ai-is-in-their-daily-lives/), the rules stipulate that individuals must be informed when they interact with artificial intelligence (AI) or view AI-generated or edited content. This move is designed to promote transparency and trust in AI systems, but it may also lead to "disclosure fatigue" among users.

## Why It Matters to Developers

For developers, these new rules have significant implications. Companies will need to implement systems to detect and disclose AI-generated content, which can be a complex task. This may require significant investments in infrastructure and personnel to ensure compliance. Moreover, the rules may lead to a shift in how AI is perceived by the general public, with potential consequences for the development and deployment of AI systems.
