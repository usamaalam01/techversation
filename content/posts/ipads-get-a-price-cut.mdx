## Introduction

In a move that's likely to delight developers and consumers alike, Apple's iPads have seen a price cut after a recent hike. According to a report by [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/gadgets/969003/apple-ipad-air-pro-airtag-deal-sale), the 13-inch iPad Pro M5 with 512GB of storage is now available for $150 off at Amazon, bringing the price down to $1,549. This deal is a welcome relief for developers who rely on these devices for their work.

## Why it Matters

For developers, the iPad is more than just a consumer device. It's a powerful tool for testing and debugging apps, as well as a platform for creating content. The iPad's portability and versatility make it an ideal device for working on the go. However, the recent price hike had made it less accessible to many developers. The current deal is a chance for them to get their hands on a high-end iPad without breaking the bank.
