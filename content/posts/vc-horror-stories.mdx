## Introduction
A recent Twitter thread by @eastdakota, shared on Hacker News, has brought to light three of the worst VC stories, sending shockwaves through the startup community. The thread, which can be found at https://twitter.com/eastdakota/status/2062860530360959273, has sparked a necessary conversation about the darker side of venture capital funding. As developers and founders, it's essential to understand the implications of these stories and how they can impact our own journeys.

## The Stories
The three stories shared in the thread are a stark reminder of the power imbalance that can exist between founders and VCs. From broken promises to manipulative tactics, these stories highlight the importance of founder preparedness and due diligence when dealing with VCs. For instance, one of the stories mentions a VC firm that promised to invest in a startup, only to pull out at the last minute, leaving the founders in a precarious financial situation.

### Due Diligence
So, what can founders do to protect themselves from such situations? The answer lies in thorough due diligence. Founders must research the VC firm, its investment history, and its reputation in the industry. They should also be cautious of VCs who make promises that seem too good to be true or who use high-pressure sales tactics. By being prepared and doing their homework, founders can reduce the risk of falling prey to unscrupulous VCs.

## Why It Matters
These stories matter because they have significant implications for the startup ecosystem. The power dynamic between founders and VCs can be skewed, with VCs holding the purse strings and founders desperate for funding. This imbalance can lead to exploitation, as seen in the stories shared in the thread. Furthermore, these stories can also deter founders from seeking funding, which can stifle innovation and hinder the growth of the startup ecosystem.
