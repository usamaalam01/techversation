## Introduction to Earth AI's Move

Earth AI, a company focused on using artificial intelligence to search for critical minerals, has decided to [vertically integrate](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/29/earth-ai-is-vertically-integrating-the-search-for-critical-minerals/) its operations. This move comes after the company faced months-long delays in its search efforts. By taking matters into its own hands, Earth AI aims to streamline its processes and reduce reliance on external factors.

## Why it Matters to Developers

The decision by Earth AI to vertically integrate its search for critical minerals has significant implications for developers, particularly those working in the tech industry. Critical minerals such as lithium, cobalt, and rare earth elements are essential components in the production of various technologies, including [electric vehicle batteries](https://www.iea.org/topics/electric-vehicles/), [renewable energy systems](https://www.iea.org/topics/renewables/), and [advanced electronics](https://www.iea.org/topics/digitalisation/). A stable and efficient supply chain for these minerals is crucial for the development and manufacturing of these technologies.

```python
# Example of a simple supply chain model
class Mineral:
    def __init__(self, name, quantity):
        self.name = name
        self.quantity = quantity

class SupplyChain:
    def __init__(self):
        self.minerals = []

    def add_mineral(self, mineral):
        self.minerals.append(mineral)

    def get_mineral_quantity(self, name):
        for mineral in self.minerals:
            if mineral.name == name:
                return mineral.quantity
        return 0

# Create a supply chain and add minerals
supply_chain = SupplyChain()
supply_chain.add_mineral(Mineral("Lithium", 1000))
supply_chain.add_mineral(Mineral("Cobalt", 500))

# Get the quantity of a mineral
lithium_quantity = supply_chain.get_mineral_quantity("Lithium")
print(f"Lithium quantity: {lithium_quantity}")
```
