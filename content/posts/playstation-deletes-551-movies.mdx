## Introduction

The news that PlayStation is deleting 551 movies from customers' accounts has sent shockwaves through the gaming and entertainment communities. According to a report by [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/playstation-store-movies-digital-studio-canal-terminator-2000711013), the move is due to the expiration of a licensing agreement between PlayStation and StudioCanal, a European film production and distribution company. This incident has raised important questions about digital ownership, licensing agreements, and the rights of consumers in the digital age.

## What Happened

The deleted movies include titles such as "The Terminator" and "The Hunger Games," which were previously available for purchase on the PlayStation Store. Customers who had purchased these movies are now finding that they are no longer available to stream or download, despite having paid for them. This has led to widespread frustration and disappointment among PlayStation users, who feel that they have been stripped of their rightful ownership of the digital content.
