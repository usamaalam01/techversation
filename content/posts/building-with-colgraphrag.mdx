## Introduction
Graph-grounded multimodal question answering has gained significant attention in recent years, as it enables the organization of text, tables, and images in a structured evidence graph. However, the accuracy of these models depends on the ranking of multimodal assets, which can lead to the discard of important patch- and token-level structure. To address this issue, ColGraphRAG has been proposed as a novel approach for late-interaction evidence retrieval in multimodal GraphRAG.

## Background
Before diving into the implementation of ColGraphRAG, let's briefly review the concept of graph-grounded multimodal question answering. In this setting, a question is asked about a given context, which consists of text, tables, and images. The goal is to answer the question by retrieving relevant evidence from the context.

## ColGraphRAG Architecture
The ColGraphRAG architecture consists of three main components:
1. **Graph Construction**: The first step is to construct a graph that represents the relationships between the different modalities (text, tables, and images).
2. **Node Representation**: Once the graph is constructed, each node is represented using a combination of token-level and patch-level features.
3. **Evidence Retrieval**: The final step is to retrieve the relevant evidence from the graph, which is done using a late-interaction mechanism.
