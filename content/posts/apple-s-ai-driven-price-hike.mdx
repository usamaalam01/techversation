## Introduction to the Price Hike

Recently, Apple announced price increases on several of its products, including the 16-inch MacBook Pro, the 11-inch iPad Air, and the HomePod Mini. According to a report by [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/report/958678/apple-consumer-price-increase-ai-big-tech), these price hikes are a result of the company's significant investment in AI technology. As developers, it's essential to understand the implications of this move and how it may affect our work.
