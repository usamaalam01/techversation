## What is Self-Hosting?
Self-hosting refers to the practice of hosting and managing one's own online presence, rather than relying on third-party services. This can include hosting websites, email servers, and other online applications. Self-hosting gives individuals and organizations more control over their online presence and allows them to avoid relying on large corporations or centralized services. ![a computer with a keyboard and mouse](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1669023414162-5bb06bbff0ec?crop=entropy&cs=tinysrgb&fit=max&fm=jpg&ixid=M3w5MzY4MzJ8MHwxfHNlYXJjaHwyfHxzZWxmJTIwbmV3JTIwdG9wJTIwbGV2ZWwlMjBkb21haW4lMjB0ZWNobm9sb2d5JTIwc29mdHdhcmV8ZW58MXwwfHx8MTc4Mjc5MjkyM3ww&ixlib=rb-4.1.0&q=80&w=1080)
