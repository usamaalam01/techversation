## Introduction to Video Indexing

The ability to index and analyze large volumes of video data has long been a topic of interest in the tech community. With the rise of AI and machine learning, developers have been exploring new ways to efficiently process and understand video content. A recent post on [Hacker News](https://blog.simbastack.com/indexed-a-year-of-video-locally/) has sparked significant attention, as a developer has successfully indexed a year's worth of video locally on a 2021 MacBook using Gemma4-31B.

## The Achievement

The achievement is notable not only because of the sheer volume of data being indexed but also due to the hardware used. The developer managed to accomplish this feat using a 2021 MacBook, which is not typically considered a high-performance machine for such tasks. The use of Gemma4-31B, a large language model, enabled the developer to leverage the power of AI to efficiently index the video data. This was made possible by utilizing 50GB of swap space, which allowed the model to process the large amounts of data.
