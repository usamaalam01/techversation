## Introduction to HalluSquatting

A recent report by Ars Technica has revealed that hackers can utilize 9 of the most popular AI tools to assemble massive botnets. This exploit, dubbed HalluSquatting, exploits the limitations of large language models (LLMs) in handling uncertain or unknown information. By leveraging the LLMs' inability to say "I don't know," hackers can manipulate these models to generate malicious code, ultimately leading to the creation of substantial botnets.

## What is HalluSquatting?

HalluSquatting is a novel attack vector that targets the weaknesses of LLMs. These models are designed to generate human-like text based on the input they receive. However, when faced with uncertain or unknown information, they often struggle to respond accurately. Instead of saying "I don't know," they may generate text that is incorrect or misleading. Hackers can exploit this behavior to create malicious code, which can then be used to assemble botnets.
