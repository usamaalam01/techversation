## The Refining Process

To understand the significance of oil refineries, it's essential to grasp the basics of the refining process. Oil refineries are complex facilities that convert crude oil into various petroleum products, such as gasoline, diesel, and jet fuel. The process involves several stages, including:

* Crude oil intake and storage
* Separation and purification of crude oil components
* Conversion of heavy crude oil into lighter products
* Treatment and blending of final products

The article on Construction Physics provides a detailed explanation of these stages, using diagrams and illustrations to help readers visualize the process. For developers, understanding the refining process can be beneficial when working on projects related to energy management, industrial automation, or environmental monitoring.
