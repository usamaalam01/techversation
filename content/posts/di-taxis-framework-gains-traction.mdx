The Diátaxis framework, recently discussed on Hacker News, has been making waves in the developer community. ## What is Diátaxis?
Diátaxis is a framework for creating documentation that emphasizes the importance of understanding the user's goals and context. It was introduced by Daniele Procida, who argues that traditional documentation often falls short in providing useful information to users. The framework is based on four modes of documentation:

* Tutorials: step-by-step guides for completing a specific task
* How-to guides: practical instructions for achieving a particular goal
* Reference materials: detailed information about a specific topic or technology
* Explanatory content: in-depth explanations of concepts and ideas

By focusing on these four modes, Diátaxis aims to provide a more comprehensive and user-friendly approach to documentation.
