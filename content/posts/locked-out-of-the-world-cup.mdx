The 2026 World Cup is expected to be a global celebration of soccer, bringing together fans from all over the world. ## The Visa Process
The visa process for fans from Arab countries is complex and often unpredictable. The [Wired article](https://www.wired.com/story/locked-out-world-cup-america-visas-fans/) highlights the challenges faced by fans from countries such as Egypt, Morocco, and Tunisia. These fans often have to wait for months or even years to get a visa, and even then, there are no guarantees of approval. This has led to frustration and disappointment among fans who are eager to attend the World Cup.

### The Role of Technology
Technology can play a crucial role in streamlining the visa process and making it more efficient. For example, online platforms can be used to submit visa applications, reducing the need for physical paperwork and minimizing the risk of errors. Additionally, machine learning algorithms can be used to analyze visa applications and identify potential security risks, making the process more secure and efficient.

```python
import pandas as pd

# Sample visa application data
data = {
    'name': ['John Doe', 'Jane Smith', 'Bob Johnson'],
    'country': ['USA', 'Canada', 'Mexico'],
    'purpose': ['Tourism', 'Business', 'Education']
}

# Create a pandas dataframe
df = pd.DataFrame(data)

# Use machine learning algorithms to analyze visa applications
from sklearn.ensemble import RandomForestClassifier
from sklearn.model_selection import train_test_split

# Split the data into training and testing sets
X_train, X_test, y_train, y_test = train_test_split(df.drop('purpose', axis=1), df['purpose'], test_size=0.2, random_state=42)

# Train a random forest classifier
clf = RandomForestClassifier(n_estimators=100, random_state=42)
clf.fit(X_train, y_train)

# Evaluate the model
print(clf.score(X_test, y_test))
```
