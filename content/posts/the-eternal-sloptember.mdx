## What Happened
Geohot's post, [The Eternal Sloptember](https://geohot.github.io//blog/jekyll/update/2026/05/24/the-eternal-sloptember.html), argues that the software development industry has become too focused on quick fixes and temporary solutions. This approach, dubbed "Sloptember," leads to complex and unmaintainable codebases that are difficult to work with. The post sparked a lively discussion on [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/), with many developers sharing their own experiences with Sloptember-like projects.

The issues raised by Geohot are essential for developers to consider. As software projects grow in complexity, maintainability becomes a significant concern. Developers must prioritize maintainability and simplicity in their projects to avoid the pitfalls of Sloptember.
