## Introduction to the Controversy

A recent [European citizens' initiative](https://citizens-initiative.europa.eu/initiatives/details/2026/000011_en) has sparked a heated debate about the future of the internet. The initiative, titled "Stop Killing the Internet: No Digital ID and No Age Verification," aims to prevent the implementation of digital IDs and age verification measures on the internet. This movement has gained significant traction, with many arguing that such regulations would undermine users' right to anonymity and freedom of expression.

## Why It Matters to Developers

As developers, it's essential to understand the implications of these regulations on our work and the internet as a whole. The proposed measures would require websites and online services to verify users' ages and identities, potentially using digital IDs. This could lead to a significant increase in the amount of personal data collected and stored online, raising concerns about data protection and privacy.

While the intention behind these measures might be to protect minors and prevent online harm, the consequences could be far-reaching. Developers would need to adapt their systems to comply with these regulations, potentially requiring significant changes to their architecture and infrastructure.

The community sentiment around this issue is divided. Some argue that digital IDs and age verification are necessary to ensure a safe and secure online environment, while others believe that these measures would infringe upon users' rights and freedoms.

* Restrictions on online anonymity and freedom of expression

* Increased costs and complexity for developers and online services
