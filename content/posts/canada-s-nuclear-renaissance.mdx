## Introduction

Canada has announced plans to embark on a "nuclear renaissance" by building up to 10 new nuclear reactors by 2040. This ambitious project aims to reduce the country's reliance on fossil fuels and lower greenhouse gas emissions. The news has sparked a mix of reactions from the public, with some hailing it as a crucial step towards a sustainable future, while others have raised concerns about the safety and environmental implications of nuclear energy.
