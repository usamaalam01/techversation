## Introduction to the Deal

In November last year, Snap announced a [$400M deal with Perplexity](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/06/snap-says-its-400m-deal-with-perplexity-amicably-ended/), a company that specializes in AI-powered search engines. The deal would have seen Perplexity's search engine integrated directly into Snapchat, marking a significant move by Snap to enhance its platform's search capabilities. However, in a surprising turn of events, Snap has now announced that the deal has ended amicably, citing changed priorities.
