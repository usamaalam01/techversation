## Introduction to Meta's New Policy
In a significant development, Meta has announced that its workers can now opt out of being tracked at work for up to 30 minutes. This policy change, as reported by the [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c93x0k194yno), is a notable shift in the company's approach to employee monitoring. The move is likely to be seen as a positive step towards improving worker privacy and trust.

## Why It Matters to Developers
For developers, this policy change matters for several reasons. Firstly, it highlights the growing importance of privacy and data protection in the workplace. As technology continues to advance, employers are increasingly using digital tools to monitor employee activity. However, this can often lead to concerns about surveillance and the potential for abuse. By giving workers the option to opt out of tracking, Meta is acknowledging the need for a balance between productivity and privacy.

```python
# Example of a simple employee tracking system
class Employee:
    def __init__(self, name, id):
        self.name = name
        self.id = id
        self.tracking_status = True

    def opt_out(self):
        self.tracking_status = False

# Create an employee object
employee = Employee("John Doe", 123)

# Opt out of tracking
employee.opt_out()
print(employee.tracking_status)  # Output: False
```
