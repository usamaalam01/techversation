## What are Fecal Transplants?
Fecal transplants, also known as fecal microbiota transplants, involve the transfer of fecal matter from a healthy donor into the gut of an individual with a compromised gut microbiome. The goal of this treatment is to restore the balance of the gut microbiome, which has been linked to various health conditions, including autism.

```python
import pandas as pd
from sklearn.model_selection import train_test_split
from sklearn.ensemble import RandomForestClassifier

# Load data from fecal transplant study
data = pd.read_csv('fecal_transplant_data.csv')

# Split data into training and testing sets
X_train, X_test, y_train, y_test = train_test_split(data.drop('outcome', axis=1), data['outcome'], test_size=0.2, random_state=42)

# Train random forest classifier to predict outcome of fecal transplant
rfc = RandomForestClassifier(n_estimators=100, random_state=42)
rfc.fit(X_train, y_train)

# Evaluate model performance
accuracy = rfc.score(X_test, y_test)
print(f'Model accuracy: {accuracy:.3f}')
```
