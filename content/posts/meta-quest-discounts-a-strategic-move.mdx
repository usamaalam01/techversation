## Introduction to Meta's Discounts

In a bid to increase adoption and drive sales, Meta has introduced promo codes and coupons for its virtual reality (VR) products, including the Meta Quest 3 and Ray-Ban AI glasses. According to a report by [Wired](https://www.wired.com/story/meta-quest-promo-code/), customers can save up to 20% on the latest games and devices. This move is significant, as it indicates a shift in Meta's strategy to make its VR products more accessible to a wider audience.
