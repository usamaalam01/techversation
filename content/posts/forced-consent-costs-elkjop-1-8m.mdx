## Introduction

A recent fine of €1.8M imposed on Elkjop, a Norwegian electronics retailer, has brought attention to the issue of forced consent in data collection. The fine is a result of a 5-year investigation, which found that the company had been using unlawful forced consent practices to collect user data. This incident serves as a warning to companies to prioritize user privacy and highlights the importance of obtaining genuine user consent.

## What Happened

According to the investigation, Elkjop had been using a tactic known as "forced consent" to collect user data. This involves presenting users with a consent form that is difficult to refuse, often by making it a requirement for accessing a service or feature. In Elkjop's case, the company had been using this tactic to collect user data for marketing purposes, without properly informing users about how their data would be used.

The investigation found that Elkjop's forced consent practices were unlawful and violated the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The company had been using a variety of tactics to obtain user consent, including pre-ticked boxes and complex language that made it difficult for users to understand what they were agreeing to.

## Why it Matters to Developers

The Elkjop fine is a significant development for developers, as it highlights the importance of prioritizing user privacy in data collection. Developers have a responsibility to ensure that their applications and services are collecting user data in a way that is transparent, fair, and lawful.

To avoid similar fines, developers should focus on obtaining genuine user consent, rather than relying on forced consent tactics. This can be achieved by using clear and simple language in consent forms, making it easy for users to refuse consent, and providing users with control over their data.

### Best Practices for Obtaining User Consent

To obtain genuine user consent, developers should follow these best practices:

* Use clear and simple language in consent forms
* Make it easy for users to refuse consent
* Provide users with control over their data
* Avoid using pre-ticked boxes or complex language
* Ensure that consent forms are accessible and easy to understand

## Broader Implications

The Elkjop fine has broader implications for the tech industry, highlighting the need for companies to prioritize user privacy and ethics in data collection. The incident raises questions about the ethics of forced consent and the impact it can have on users.
