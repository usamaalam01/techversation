## What are Geofence Warrants?

Geofence warrants are a type of digital search warrant that allows law enforcement agencies to collect data from devices within a specific geographic area. This data can include location information, IP addresses, and other identifying details. Geofence warrants have become increasingly popular in recent years, particularly in cases involving protests, demonstrations, and other large gatherings.

## The Supreme Court's Decision

The Supreme Court's ruling is a response to the growing use of geofence warrants by law enforcement agencies. The court has decided that geofence warrants require constitutional protections, which means that law enforcement agencies must demonstrate probable cause and obtain a warrant before collecting data from devices in a specific area. This decision is a significant development in the intersection of technology and law, and it has major implications for developers and the broader tech industry.
