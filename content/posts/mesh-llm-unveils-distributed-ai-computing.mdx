## What is Distributed AI Computing?

Distributed AI computing refers to the practice of splitting AI workloads across multiple machines or nodes, enabling the processing of large amounts of data in parallel. This approach allows for faster training times, improved model accuracy, and increased scalability. By distributing the computational workload, developers can build more complex AI models, handle larger datasets, and reduce the overall cost of AI development.

## Mesh LLM's Approach

Mesh LLM's distributed AI computing system on iroh is designed to provide a seamless and efficient way for developers to build, deploy, and manage AI models. The system utilizes a mesh architecture, where multiple nodes are connected to form a network, allowing for the distribution of computational tasks. This approach enables developers to take advantage of the collective processing power of the network, resulting in faster and more efficient AI model development.

### Key Features of Mesh LLM's System

* **Scalability**: Mesh LLM's system allows for horizontal scaling, enabling developers to easily add or remove nodes as needed, depending on the size of the workload.
* **Flexibility**: The system supports a wide range of AI frameworks and libraries, providing developers with the flexibility to choose the tools that best suit their needs.
* **Security**: Mesh LLM's system incorporates robust security features, ensuring the integrity and confidentiality of sensitive data.
