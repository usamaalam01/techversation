## Introduction to the Issue

A recent study published in the [American Journal of Public Health](https://ajph.aphapublications.org/doi/10.2105/AJPH.2026.308501) has shed light on the alarming presence of ultra-processed foods in the global food system, with tobacco companies playing a significant role in their production and distribution. This revelation has sparked intense debate and raised important questions about the intersection of technology, public health, and the food industry. As developers and tech practitioners, it's essential to examine the implications of this issue and consider how our work contributes to the broader landscape of food systems.

## The Role of Tobacco Companies

The study's findings indicate that tobacco companies, known for their harmful impact on public health, have diversified their investments to include the production and marketing of ultra-processed foods. These foods, characterized by their high levels of added sugars, salt, and unhealthy fats, have been linked to various health problems, including obesity, diabetes, and heart disease. The involvement of tobacco companies in the food industry is particularly concerning, as it suggests a deliberate effort to prioritize profits over public health.
