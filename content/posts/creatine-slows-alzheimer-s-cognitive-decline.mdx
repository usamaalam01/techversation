## Introduction to Creatine and Alzheimer's

A recent study published on [The SciVerse](https://thesciverse.org/scientists-found-that-the-creatine-supplement-millions-take-for-muscle-gains-is-quietly-raising-brain-energy-levels-and-slowing-early-alzheimers-cognitive-decline-by-30/) has found that creatine supplements can raise brain energy levels and slow cognitive decline in early Alzheimer's patients by 30%. This discovery has significant implications for the treatment and prevention of Alzheimer's disease, a condition that affects millions of people worldwide.

## What is Creatine?

Creatine is a naturally occurring substance found in the body, primarily in muscle tissue. It is also available as a dietary supplement, commonly used by athletes to improve muscle mass and endurance. Creatine works by increasing the amount of phosphocreatine in the muscles, which is used to produce ATP (adenosine triphosphate), the energy currency of the body.
