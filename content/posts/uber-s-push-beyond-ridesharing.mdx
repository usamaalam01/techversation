## Introduction to Uber's Expansion

Uber has long been known for its ride-hailing services, but the company has always had ambitions to be more than just a ride. With its latest moves, Uber is accelerating its push into new areas, including autonomous vehicles (AVs) and data provision. According to a recent report by [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/05/10/uber-has-always-wanted-to-be-more-than-a-ride-now-it-has-reason-to-hurry/), Uber's strategy involves embedding itself in the AV industry as a data provider, investor, and distribution platform.

### Autonomous Vehicles and Data Provision

Uber's push into AVs is driven by the potential for autonomous vehicles to revolutionize the transportation sector. With its investments in the AV industry, Uber aims to position itself as a key player in the emerging market. By providing access to large datasets, Uber can help accelerate the development of autonomous vehicles and other transportation technologies.
