## Introduction to Every Eval Ever Results

Hugging Face, a leading platform for machine learning model development and sharing, has announced the inclusion of Every Eval Ever results on their model pages. This update is a significant step forward in promoting model transparency and facilitating more informed decision-making among developers. Every Eval Ever is an initiative that aims to provide a comprehensive overview of a model's performance across various benchmarks and evaluation metrics.

## Why It Matters to Developers

The introduction of Every Eval Ever results on Hugging Face model pages is a game-changer for developers. It allows them to compare models more effectively, making it easier to choose the best model for their specific use case. Previously, developers had to rely on limited information about a model's performance, which often made it difficult to determine its suitability for a particular task. With Every Eval Ever results, developers can now access a wealth of information about a model's performance, including its strengths and weaknesses.
