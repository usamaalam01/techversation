## Introduction to Dopamine Fracking

The concept of dopamine fracking has been making waves in the tech community, particularly after being discussed on [Hacker News](https://igerman.cc/blog/dopamine-fracking/). But what exactly is dopamine fracking, and why does it matter to developers? In essence, dopamine fracking refers to the practice of exploiting psychological vulnerabilities in humans to keep them engaged with a product or service. This can be done through various techniques, such as variable rewards, social validation, and endless scrolling.

## Why it Matters to Developers

As developers, it's essential to understand the implications of dopamine fracking on our work. When we design and build products, we often aim to create an engaging user experience. However, this can sometimes lead to the exploitation of psychological vulnerabilities, even if unintentionally. For instance, a well-designed variable reward system can keep users coming back for more, but it can also lead to addiction and negative consequences on mental health.
