## Introduction

In a move that is being closely watched by the tech industry and beyond, Minnesota has become the first state to ban prediction markets. This development is significant, as it marks a major shift in the way that governments are approaching the regulation of emerging technologies. In this post, we will summarize what happened, explain why it matters to developers, and provide our take on the broader implications of this move.

## What Happened

According to a report by [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/05/19/nx-s1-5821265/minnesota-ban-prediction-markets), the ban on prediction markets was enacted in response to concerns over their potential impact on society. Prediction markets are online platforms that allow users to bet on the outcome of future events, such as elections, sports games, and even the weather. While proponents of prediction markets argue that they provide a valuable tool for predicting future outcomes, critics argue that they can be used for illicit purposes, such as insider trading and manipulation of financial markets.
