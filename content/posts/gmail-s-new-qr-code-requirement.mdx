## Introduction to Gmail's New Requirement

Gmail, one of the most widely used email services, has recently introduced a new requirement for registering a new account. As reported on [Hacker News](https://discuss.privacyguides.net/t/google-account-registration-now-requires-sending-an-sms-via-phone-instead-of-receiving-an-sms/36082), users are now required to scan a QR code and send a text message as part of the registration process. This change has sparked a heated debate among developers and users about the implications of this new requirement.

## Broader Implications

The introduction of this new requirement raises concerns about user privacy and the potential for increased friction in the registration process. On one hand, the use of QR code scanning and texting can provide an additional layer of security against automated bots and spam accounts. On the other hand, it may also create barriers for users who do not have access to a smartphone or prefer not to use texting services.
