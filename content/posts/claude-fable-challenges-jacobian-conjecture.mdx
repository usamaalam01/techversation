## What is the Jacobian Conjecture?

The Jacobian Conjecture is a problem in algebraic geometry that deals with the behavior of polynomial maps. In essence, it states that if a polynomial map has a non-zero Jacobian determinant, then it is invertible. This conjecture has far-reaching implications for many areas of mathematics, including algebra, geometry, and analysis. Despite considerable effort by mathematicians over the years, a proof or counterexample to the Jacobian Conjecture had remained elusive until Claude Fable's recent claim.
