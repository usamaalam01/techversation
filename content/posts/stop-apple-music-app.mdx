## Introduction to the Problem
The Apple Music app can be frustrating when it launches automatically, disrupting the user experience. This issue has been a topic of discussion on platforms like [Hacker News](https://lowtechguys.com/musicdecoy/), where users share their grievances and potential solutions. One such solution is the musicdecoy tool, which allows users to prevent the Apple Music app from launching.

## What is musicdecoy?
Musicdecoy is a simple tool that creates a decoy Apple Music app, tricking the operating system into thinking the app is already running. This decoy app prevents the actual Apple Music app from launching, giving users more control over their device. The tool is easy to use and requires minimal technical expertise.
