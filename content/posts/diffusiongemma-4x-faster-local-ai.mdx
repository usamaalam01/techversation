## What is Diffusion AI?

Diffusion AI is a type of generative model that uses a process called diffusion-based image synthesis. This process involves iteratively refining a random noise signal until it converges to a specific image. Diffusion AI has been used in various applications, including image generation, image editing, and even text-to-image synthesis.
