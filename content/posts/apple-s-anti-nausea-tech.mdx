## What Happened

The anti-nausea dots are a part of Apple's vehicle motion cues, a feature designed to help reduce motion sickness in cars. The technology uses a combination of visual cues, including a series of dots that appear on the screen, to help the brain better understand the motion of the vehicle. According to the article, the feature has been found to be highly effective, with many users reporting a significant reduction in car sickness symptoms.
