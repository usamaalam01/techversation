## Introduction to Conditional Attribute Estimation

Conditional attribute estimation is a technique used in natural language processing and other sequence modeling tasks to estimate sequence-level properties. These properties can include attributes such as sentiment, topic, or style, which are not necessarily tied to individual tokens but rather to the sequence as a whole. Traditional next-token prediction models often struggle with these tasks, as they focus on local patterns and may not capture global structure.

## Challenges of Next-Token Prediction

Next-token prediction is a common objective in sequence modeling, where the model is trained to predict the next token in a sequence given the context of the previous tokens. However, this approach can lead to overfitting of local patterns and underfitting of global structure. For example, a model may learn to predict the next word in a sentence based on the previous words, but it may not capture the overall sentiment or topic of the sentence.

## Autoregressive Sequence Models

Autoregressive sequence models are a type of model that can effectively estimate sequence-level properties. These models work by predicting the next token in a sequence based on the previous tokens, but they also take into account the global structure of the sequence. This is achieved through the use of a conditional probability distribution, which models the probability of each token given the context of the previous tokens.

        self.rnn = nn.LSTM(hidden_size, hidden_size, num_layers, batch_first=True)
